# Stage 18 — fourfold cross-validated scalar spectroscopy audit

## Sources and integrity
Eight Stage 18 scalar-continuation ZIPs, four each for square and Penrose seeds 101, 211, 307, 419. Each contains 20 consecutive 100-sweep segments, with 10 time-slice observations per segment. Data are 200 gauge configurations per seed, timeslices 20, channels 12. Continuity and last-hash checks were performed in the preceding Stage 18 integrity audit (`Stage18_Scalar_12000_Initial_Audit.zip`). This analysis independently validates segment numbering and array shape before use. The eight chain datasets are not eight independent ensembles from Stage 17: Stage 18 starts from saved Stage 9 states and common RNG streams.

## Prespecified analysis
For each geometry and heldout seed, use only the other three chains to estimate the full 12x12 zero-lag connected operator covariance. Retain up to three largest C(0) modes with eigenvalues >1e-5 of the leading eigenvalue; form frozen PCA directions. Independently, perform a regularized C(1)/C(0) generalized eigenproblem on the training covariance-stable subspace and retain the three largest eigenvalue directions. APE level 12 is tested alongside levels 0, 2, 6. Project the untouched heldout data onto the frozen operator vectors. Compute periodic, timeslice-averaged, vacuum-subtracted correlators C(t), t=0..6. Re-estimate the vacuum mean within every circular moving-block bootstrap replicate of the heldout chain. 300 replicates per heldout/estimator/block; blocks 10, 20, 40 configurations. Require three successive times in t=2..6 with strictly positive lower 95% percentile bounds, stable across the three block lengths and all four heldout folds, before attempting a mass fit. No estimated masses or physical continuum ratios are admitted.

## Results
- Square, PCA: zero qualifying time windows in heldouts 101, 211, 419 at all block sizes; on heldout 307, one PCA direction passes at block 40 **only**, not at 10 or 20. Consequently not robust.
- Square, generalized eigenbasis: no qualifying windows at any heldout or block size.
- Penrose, PCA or generalized eigenbasis: no qualifying windows at any heldout or block size.
- Nine-channel ablation, using the same training-only rank-three PCA and block-20 bootstrap (500 resamples): zero qualifying heldout windows for both 9 and 12 channels on either geometry. The largest SNR across folds at t=2..6 is [2.27,2.43,2.35,0.98,2.45] for square nine channels and [2.49,2.66,2.36,0.99,2.62] for square twelve channels; Penrose nine [1.88,1.86,1.83,0.99,1.49] vs twelve [1.84,1.79,1.74,0.92,1.53]. These are maxima across folded projections and cannot be interpreted as independent or corrected p-values. No robust advantage of level 12 is detected.

## Limitations and recommended physics strategy
The analysis only tests rank-three PCA and training-only truncated GEVP projections, not all possible gauge-invariant scalar operator constructions or every fit model. C(t) from a single heldout chain is noisier than a combined estimator and the four chains are not proof of thermal stationarity. The operator set contains highly correlated APE levels. Short-time plateaus or mass fits are *not* physically admitted in the absence of a crossvalidated, stable positive multi-time signal. Additional sweeps with the same 12 operators are not yet well motivated. Next design should target genuinely different gauge-invariant scalar loops with larger physical extent, varied path shape and zero-momentum averaging; compare symmetry/admissibility on Penrose, operator overlap and condition number. Consider scalar-appropriate multilevel estimators or a training/validation pilot with a very small number of configurations first. Do not transfer the successful open Wilson-loop multihit procedure directly to the vacuum-subtracted connected scalar correlator without a new conditional estimator derivation and validation.

## Reproduce
Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python analyze.py` with input ZIPs under `/mnt/data` or set `SCALAR_ZIP_ROOT`. This writes `RESULTS.json`. `compare.py` provides 9 vs 12 PCA comparison and writes `COMPARISON_9_12.json`. Both scripts preserve all input archives.
