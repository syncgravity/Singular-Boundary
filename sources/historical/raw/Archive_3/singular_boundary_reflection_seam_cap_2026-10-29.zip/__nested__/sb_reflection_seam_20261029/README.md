# Reflection-seam full-coupling SU(2) Wilson cap positivity

- `PROOF_REPORT.md`: standalone Hamiltonian positive-Hilbert-Schmidt cone proof, symmetry assumptions, polynomial cap and magnetic deletion corollaries.
- `verify_reflection_seam.py`: deterministic SU(2) Gram, positive-cone Trotter, exact 3×3 reflection geometry, and Haar-cap test. Requires NumPy and SciPy.
- `VERIFY_LOG.txt`: reproducible output.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: per-file hashes.

This theorem **requires exact spatial reflection pairing of all electric and magnetic couplings**. It does not prove cap survival for arbitrary non-reflection-symmetric Penrose interiors, or a continuum mass gap.
