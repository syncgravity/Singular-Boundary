# One-sided exact deleted-face fiber-potential budget

Theorem: For the actual finite nonlinear magnetic-deleted SU(2) ground state, the positive part of the exact conditional energy above its ground energy controls the amount its radial marginal can increase from a center to the equator. No single-crossing or fast transverse gap is needed.

The total budget B_f=(4/A_f) integral_0^(pi/2) sin(u)cos(u)[U_f(cos u)-E_f^-]_+ du implies deleted near-center mass >= exp(-2 B_f) Haar cap mass, and the nonlinear deletion increment <= C h^-1(1+log(1/h)+B_f).

The open question is proving B_f=o(h^-1/4) uniformly on genuine cycle-rich Penrose interior faces. Conditional on this, one obtains fixed-support Wilson-angle source norm equivalence. This package does not prove the missing budget, Yang-Mills mass gap, or continuum reconstruction.
