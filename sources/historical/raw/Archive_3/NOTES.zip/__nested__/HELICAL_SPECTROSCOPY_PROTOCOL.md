# The Singular Boundary — Stage 27: pre-registered helical-observer spectroscopy
**Research protocol, 26 October 2026. Status: prospective, not a measured mass or demonstration of additional glueball states.**

## 1. Paper 3 source and the hypothesis
Sharpe, *The Complex Structure of Quasicrystalline Spacetime: The J-Operator and the Hidden Domain*, §7.1–7.3, proposes constant-amplitude trajectories `z₂(τ)=A exp(2πipτ)`, with Conway-worm-commensurate pitches **p=3/13, 5/13, 8/13** and a dominant perpendicular-space angular mode **m=10**. Remark 6 calls the observer effect **kinematic, not dynamical**. Its quoted 3:5:8 is therefore a pitch/frequency result **for sampling of a static geometric field**, not a Yang–Mills mass result. In the discrete, once-per-step convention, frequencies `10p` alias modulo 1; the three pitch values are not distinguishable as 3:5:8 frequencies from such samples. An actual gauge-theory spectral test must use the Euclidean transfer-time direction independently and measure transfer correlators, not helix sampling oscillation frequencies.

## 2. Exact operational definitions
On a *canonical, explicitly declared* Penrose cut-and-project graph, compute for each spatial anchor `x` its perpendicular-space lift `z₂(x)` and a **geometrically independently constructed** integer Conway-worm coordinate `k(x)` (mod 13). Their origin/phase conventions and five-dimensional basis must be recorded; neither `x`'s array index nor Euclidean time is a valid replacement for `k(x)`. `z₂` must be taken from the actual model set, not reconstructed from a 2D picture when phase matters. Pre-register the median window radius `r₀`, width `σ_r`, angular concentration `κ=3`, phase origin `θ₀=0`, angular harmonic `m=10` and the worm family whose index is used.

For pitch `p=n/13`, define a nonnegative, fixed, time-independent weight

`b_n(x)=exp{κ[cos(10(arg z₂(x)−2π n k(x)/13−θ₀))−1]} exp{−(|z₂(x)|−r₀)²/(2σ_r²)}`,

`w_n(x)=a_x b_n(x)/Σ_y a_y b_n(y)`, where `a_x` is a predeclared positive spatial anchor measure (and not a fitted gauge observable). Set `O_n(t)=Σ_x w_n(x) ℓ_x(t)`, where `ℓ_x(t)=1/2 ReTr W_x(t)` for an existing **closed** spatial SU(2) Wilson loop catalogue. Maintain `O_full(t)=Σ_x a_x ℓ_x(t)/Σ_y a_y`, using every anchor. Define `O_n` for n=3,5,8. These are gauge invariant because weights do not depend on links, and they are ordinary fixed-spatial operators evolving in actual Euclidean time. They are NOT an exact 5-fold symmetry projection of the M55 periodic approximant. The weight prescription is **a new model choice** motivated by Paper 3, not an equation already derived in that paper.

`k(x)` orientation, radius choice and window dependence are part of the preregistration; alternative worm orientations become explicit systematic variations. The effective anchor number `N_eff=1/Σ_x w(x)^2` must be reported. If weighted support is too small or grossly nonuniform among local environments, label that operator inadmissible, rather than silently interpreting its noise as physics.

## 3. Pre-registered controls and null tests
- **Primary baseline:** the unchanged full-volume 3-,5-,8-face Stage 19 controls and the complete strict target-matched auxiliary. Keep the original scalar basis separate from the pitch-dependent test.
- **Pitch controls:** n=1,2,4 over denominator 13, and independently predeclared phase-shuffled and worm-index-shuffled versions. Match `N_eff`, radial distribution, and shape-family distributions when comparing precision.
- **Geometric null:** rerandomize `k(x)` within radial/local-environment strata, preserving worm-label histograms; determine whether pitch ordering adds predictive information beyond the selected radial ring and ordinary smoothing.
- **Symmetry/orbit caveat:** p=3/13,5/13,8/13 each permutes all thirteen residues as k runs over a complete cycle. Any score depending only on the multiset of sampled phases is necessarily pitch-blind. Pair the phases with the actual local Penrose geometry and use distinct out-of-sample predictions. This is a mathematical identifiability gate.
- **Graph controls:** literal Penrose tiling graph vs coupled Z5 graph, square/periodic and degree-matched controls; any helical effect must survive known graph-choice and weighting systematics. Square has no canonical Penrose perpendicular-space lift; do not fabricate one. Compare to full-volume square scalar channels using physical scale matching, not transplanting hidden coordinates.

## 4. Spectral estimand and falsifiable claims
For every pair of fixed operators compute the *connected, zero-spatial-momentum where appropriate* correlation matrix
`C_ij(t)=⟨O_i(t₀+t) O_j(t₀)⟩_{t₀,outer}−⟨O_i⟩⟨O_j⟩`.
For finite periodic Euclidean time extent `N_t`, fit transfer-matrix-consistent forms `Σ_{n>0} A_i^(n) A_j^(n)* [exp(−E_n t)+exp(−E_n(N_t−t))]` only in admissible windows (plus documented wrap effects/normalizations). Since weights are fixed in space, different pitches **select overlapping physical eigenstates**; they cannot be declared three new particles from distinct spectral fits unless multi-operator state isolation and independent-volume/cutoff tests establish it.

Primary falsifiable hypothesis H1 (operator quality): at comparable effective support and total CPU time, at least one of n=3,5,8 yields a smaller *outer-chain-bootstrap* SE for a resolved connected scalar plateau than the matched non-Fibonacci controls, with a held-out-chain replication. Null: no advantage after geometry/support/compute matching. H2 (spectral content): a pitch family enhances overlap with a reproducible `0++` transfer eigenstate without predicting the physical `E_n` from p. Stronger hypothesis H3 (3:5:8 spectral ratios): explicitly **exploratory, not a priori validated**, and require at least three independently isolated energy eigenvalues, a fit including non-Fibonacci competing ratios, uncertainty estimates, and a continuum check. Failures of H1/H2 do not falsify Paper 3's kinematic construction, only its hypothesized utility for Yang–Mills spectroscopy.

## 5. Stage 26 compatibility and missing data
Stage 26 archives store outer-ensemble measurements of the pre-existing four **spatially averaged** scalar channels, not per-anchor Wilson traces or `z₂/worm_index` labels per saved gauge configuration. Weighted helical `O_n(t)` **cannot be computed retrospectively** from these scalars. This is an identifiability / information-loss obstruction: a scalar average cannot determine a differently weighted spatial average. New per-anchor loop measurements on full gauge fields or a *new* measurement continuation with geometry-derived weights are necessary. Reuse the independent gauge-chain RNG/restart states, preserve chains and outer sweeps; do not relabel inner replicas as outer data. Stage 26 can serve as the **control-operator uncertainty baseline** once its matched pair and vacuum-subtraction audit is complete.

1. Confirm canonical Penrose geometry and reconstruct z₂ and Conway-worm k from the same Z5 lift, with phase and seam compatibility checks. **Stop if this is unavailable.**
2. Freeze operator definitions and hashes of weights/catalogues before touching held-out gauge data. Check closed-loop gauge invariance, time independence, full-anchor denominator and sensitivity to the M55 seam. Test rephasing origin and reflected worm directions.
3. Generate observations on the same new outer fields: each time slice stores the Stage 19 controls plus all pitch and matched-null operators. Include two inner conditional streams if feasible; if slab factorization changes, reverify that operators occupy distinct conditioned slabs and their source/sink sets are identical for direct and conditional comparisons.
4. For each geometry, bootstrap blocks within the four independent outer chains; estimate autocorrelation at fixed offset, independent-chain vacuum product, full connected covariance, inner/outer variance decomposition and SE²×runtime. Select operators and fitting windows using training chains and evaluate on excluded chains.
5. Claim helical enhancement only after predeclared effect-size thresholds, support-matched controls, and all four held-out checks; report failures and multiplicity. Claim a physical mass only when multi-t time window, GEVP conditioning, volume and cutoff checks pass. Do not infer `m/sqrt(sigma)` without admitted long-distance string tension and multiple matched cutoffs.

## 7. Transfer positivity and J
Treat the helical operator as a gauge-invariant **observable on the Hilbert space defined by the actual transfer matrix**. The construction above does not modify the Wilson measure or the temporal transfer kernel; thus it does not itself threaten reflection positivity. Conversely, it does not establish reflection positivity for the heterogeneous Stage 26 Wilson action. Defining a complex-time `J` action on the pure-gauge physical Hilbert space requires an explicit operator, positive inner product and domain; no such map is proved in Paper 3. Do not infer transfer energies or Kaluza–Klein masses from the phase weights.
