## Frustration under a physical Wilson ensemble
The fixed-scale I-A/I-B theorems require a **per-configuration deterministic** lower bound for fundamental-representation connection Laplacians. For SU(2) fundamental links, `s_min(I-H_f)` is gauge invariant for any face loop, but `U=I` satisfies all graph/geometric hypotheses and violates positive holonomy separation. A finite-volume Wilson probability measure with finite couplings has continuous strictly positive density relative to product Haar measure. Therefore **PROVED:** every open neighbourhood of the identity-link configuration has positive Gibbs probability. On such configurations every face holonomy is as close to I as desired. Hence no deterministic almost-sure, connection-independent fixed positive frustration constant delta can be asserted for finite Wilson ensembles, even on Penrose. More generally every finite large flat-region event has positive probability (but whether infinitely many occur almost surely requires an actual infinite-volume Gibbs/ergodicity argument). This blocks a direct application of I-B.3 to the unrestricted Wilson measure. At fixed finite volume a gauge-invariant *probabilistic* lower-tail statement may be possible, but cannot be confused with a volume-uniform deterministic gap. The pure-gauge physical Hilbert space is not the fundamental-spinor space of L_U.

Recommended tests: measure `s_min(I-H_f)` distribution on properly thinned **outer gauge fields** (not inner replicas); measure radius to a face exceeding each predeclared delta; count connected nearly-flat regions and growth of maximum frustration-free distance with volume at fixed a and beta; test bootstrap uncertainty preserving chain identity; ablate geometric weights and compare degree-matched periodic control. Existing Stage 26 output consists of scalar observables and restart summaries, not per-outer full link configurations, so it cannot reconstruct the per-face holonomy distribution unless links were retained.

## Exact finite-volume positive transfer construction for a DESIGN model
Let G=(V,E,F) be **any finite spatial graph**, including a Penrose patch, with compact configuration space X=SU(2)^E, Haar measure dU, and local vertex gauge group K=SU(2)^V. Let P project L²(X) orthogonally onto gauge-invariant functions, via Haar averaging. Choose a real bounded, gauge-invariant spatial plaquette potential V(U), e.g. `V(U)=sum_f alpha_f[1- (1/2)Re Tr H_f(U)]`, alpha_f >=0. On L²(X) define independent-link central *heat-kernel* convolution `C_t f(U)=int_X prod_e k_{t_e}(U_e V_e^{-1}) f(V) dV`, t_e>0. Here k_t is the SU(2) heat kernel. Each `C_t` is self-adjoint, positive **as an operator** (Peter–Weyl eigenvalues `exp[-t_e j(j+1)]`), positivity-improving in the integral-kernel sense, compact for t_e>0, and commutes with K action when endpoint gauge transformations act left and right on each link. Define `T=P M_{exp(-V/2)} C_t M_{exp(-V/2)} P` restricted to H_phys=P L²(X). Then **PROVED for this defined model:** T is compact, positive self-adjoint, gauge-invariant and nonzero. For finite connected graph with strictly positive k_t and bounded V, T is positivity-improving on nonnegative nonzero gauge-invariant functions, so its largest eigenvalue is simple and separated from the next eigenvalue (Jentzsch/Perron argument). Define H=-a_t^{-1}log(T/lambda_0) on the positive-eigenvalue sector. This gives a nonzero finite-volume excitation gap, NOT a uniform infinite-volume or continuum gap. Beware: k_t may be positive-definite as operator while positivity of kernel alone does *not* imply positive definiteness; heat-kernel Peter–Weyl coefficients are the substantive reason.

**IMPORTANT NON-EQUIVALENCE:** This is a controlled *heat-kernel temporal action*, **not** yet proof that the project's actual heterogeneous Wilson plaquette couplings induce this exact T. To prove positivity for the actual action, derive its full one-slice kernel and a character expansion with nonnegative coefficients for each timelike plaquette, verify exact temporal reflection pairing and placement of spatial action across the plane, and verify the gauge projection. Mere positivity of Boltzmann weights or a numerical cache check is insufficient. Temporal anisotropy, seam twists, M55 periodic identifications, and normalization must be matched explicitly. A general set of positive heterogeneous couplings constant in time may still permit a reflection-positive formulation; this is an **OPEN CHECK** for the exact implemented action, not a blanket assertion that heterogeneity destroys positivity.

No theorem identifies the spectrum of the above transfer operator with the fundamental-representation connection Laplacian or with the helical sampling frequencies. Such an intertwining or energy comparison must be formulated and proved, or the branches must remain distinct.
