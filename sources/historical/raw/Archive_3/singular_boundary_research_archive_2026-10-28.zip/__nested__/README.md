# The Singular Boundary — consolidated research archive (initial evidence-preserving edition)

**Snapshot:** 2026-10-28. This archive is an auditable inventory of the source material actually accessible in the current workspace, coupled with a clearly qualified reconstruction of historically retrieved conversation summaries. It is **not** a complete export of all project chats.

## Contents
- `original_uploads/`: byte-preserved copies of all 21 mounted inputs, including dissertation source package, Stage 26 archives and `.npz` data, and a surviving R3 note.
- `records/artifact_manifest.json`: checksums, sizes, inspectable array schemas, archive integrity tests.
- `records/container_member_inventory.json`: inventories of source packages and checkpoint ZIPs.
- `records/claim_register.csv`: reported claims separated from their evidentiary status.
- `records/conversation_reconstruction.csv`: branch-sequence reconstruction with coverage caveats.
- `records/missing_materials.md`: precise recovery requests and limits.
- `records/research_roadmap.md`: reconstructed scientific sequence.

## Rules for citation and verification
1. **Direct source** means a file present here; **historical summary** means a memory/retrieved account and is not a primary transcript.
2. A claim labeled *reported proved* is not independently proof-checked unless supporting proof and verification are included.
3. Computational input availability does not mean completion of downstream spectroscopy, inference, or continuum extrapolation.
4. Finite lattice, truncated SU(2) channels, and special boundary classes are not automatically valid in the infinite-volume and continuum limits.
5. SHA-256 verifies future copies against this snapshot; it does not certify historical source attribution or scientific truth.
