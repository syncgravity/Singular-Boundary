# R3 — Shared-edge recoupling: the two \(j=0,1\) channels

The next calculation is complete for the **first adjacent two-loop sector** of the four-plaquette SU(2) spatial model.

The result is encouraging: after explicitly separating the shared-edge singlet and triplet channels, their contributions to the tested three-step mixed coefficient are positive.

More importantly, we have identified precisely what this establishes—and what is still needed for a uniform large-\(\lambda\) theorem.

## 1. Exact orthonormal intertwiner states

Let \(a,b\) be adjacent plaquettes sharing one spatial edge. Define

\[
X_{ab}=\chi_{1/2}(W_a)\chi_{1/2}(W_b)
\]

and let \(O_{ab}\) denote the fundamental character of the oriented outer boundary enclosing both plaquettes.

The shared fundamental representations decompose as

\[
\frac12\otimes\frac12=0\oplus1.
\]

The normalized spin-network states are

\[
\boxed{
\Psi_{ab}^{(0)}=O_{ab},
\qquad
\Psi_{ab}^{(1)}
=\frac{2}{\sqrt3}\left(X_{ab}-\frac12O_{ab}\right).
}
\]

Their Haar inner products satisfy

\[
\langle\Psi_{ab}^{(0)},\Psi_{ab}^{(1)}\rangle=0,
\qquad
\|\Psi_{ab}^{(0)}\|=\|\Psi_{ab}^{(1)}\|=1.
\]

These are distinct eigenstates of the temporal transfer operator.

| Channel | Shared-edge spin | Other six boundary edges |
|---|---|---|
| \(j=0\) | Trivial | Fundamental |
| \(j=1\) | Adjoint | Fundamental |

The temporal multiplier is accordingly

\[
k_{ab}^{(j)}
=
k_j(t_{\mathrm{shared}})
\prod_{e\in\partial(a\cup b)}
k_{1/2}(t_e),
\]

where \(k_j(t)=I_{2j+1}(t)/I_1(t)\), with the appropriate zero-coupling limit.

## 2. The exact \(15\times15\) spatial Wilson block

We extended the previous seven states by adding both channels for each of the four adjacent plaquette pairs:

<text color="secondary" size="sm">1 vacuum + 4 single loops + 2 disjoint two-loops + 8 shared-edge channels = 15 states.</text>

The spatial multiplication matrix is

\[
F_{\alpha\beta}
=
\int f(U)\,
\overline{\Psi_\alpha(U)}\Psi_\beta(U)\,dU.
\]

Using independent plaquette Haar coordinates, SU(2) character moments, and rotational symmetry of the quaternion components, I evaluated all 225 entries—including the cross terms between the original seven states and the eight new intertwiner states.

The calculation is analytic in its SU(2) moments; the reported numerical entries come from Bessel-function evaluation.

## 4. Does positivity extend to the full system?

Not yet. We have established positivity of the selected 15-state contribution, but the complete spin-network expansion contains additional representations and intertwiners.

| Objective | Status |
|---|---|
| Shared-edge \(j=0,1\) decomposition | **Explicit** |
| Orthonormal two-channel basis | **Constructed** |
| Full \(15\times15\) Wilson multiplication block | **Computed** |
| One-loop/two-loop cross terms | **Computed** |
| Mixed coefficient for overlapping supports within this block | **Positive** |
| Complete infinite spin-network mixed sum | Open |
| Uniform control of omitted channels as \(\lambda\to\infty\) | Open |
| Arbitrary Penrose cavity insertion estimate | Open |

The positivity observed here is not automatically basis-independent entrywise positivity, nor a proof that omitted contributions cancel.

## 5. Parallel scalar lower-comparison route

We retain the target

\[
\boxed{
\frac{Z_{\mathrm{inserted}}}{Z_{\mathrm{cavity}}}
\ge
\exp[-C|Q|(1+\log\lambda)].
}
\]

For the fixed three-step twelve-prism complex, a global Haar small-ball argument supplies the weaker estimate

\[
\mathcal F_Q
\le\frac{111}{2}\log\lambda+C.
\]

This counts all 37 gauge-fixed SU(2) link variables and consequently depends on the entire finite complex. It is not the required ambient-volume-uniform bound.

The next useful theorem would replace that global count with a local conditional comparison whose cost depends only on inserted support and a bounded surrounding neighborhood.

That route remains necessary even if further finite spin-network sectors continue to contribute positively.

**Research conclusion:** The first genuinely shared-edge SU(2) intertwiner obstruction does not produce a negative mixed contribution in the tested overlapping-support example. The calculation strengthens the representation-theoretic evidence, but it has not established uniform positivity or the complete support-local insertion theorem.

I recommend using the 15-state result as a verified benchmark while moving the scalar argument toward a **localized conditional Haar-ball or correlated-transport estimate**, which addresses the large-\(\lambda\) and ambient-uniform requirements directly.
