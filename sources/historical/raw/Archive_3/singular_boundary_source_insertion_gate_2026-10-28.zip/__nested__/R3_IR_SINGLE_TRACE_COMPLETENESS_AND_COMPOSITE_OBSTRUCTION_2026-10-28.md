## 2. Theorem ST-1: explicit finite separating traces on every finite connected SU(2) gauge graph

Let G=(V,E) be a finite connected oriented graph, let r=|E|−|V|+1, and let `g_i∈SU(2)`, i=1,...,r, be the based fundamental cycle holonomies obtained by fixing a spanning-tree gauge. The residual gauge action is simultaneous conjugation.

**Statement.** The real numbers

- `Tr(g_i)` (all i);
- `Tr(g_i g_j)` (all i<j);
- `Tr(g_i g_j g_k)` (all i<j<k)

separate simultaneous SU(2) conjugacy classes on `SU(2)^r`. There are at most `r + C(r,2) + C(r,3)` such coordinates; for r=0 the physical gauge quotient is a point. Each coordinate is a fundamental Wilson trace around an actual closed graph path, with tree transport included.

**Proof.** Write `g_i=s_i I+i v_i·σ` with `s_i∈R`, `v_i∈R³`, `s_i²+|v_i|²=1`. Then

`Tr(g_i)=2s_i`,

`Tr(g_i g_j)=2(s_i s_j−v_i·v_j)`, and

`Tr(g_i g_j g_k)=2(s_i s_j s_k−s_i v_j·v_k−s_j v_i·v_k−s_k v_i·v_j+(v_i×v_j)·v_k)`.

Thus the listed traces reconstruct all scalars s_i, the Gram matrix of the v_i (diagonal entries from `1−s_i²`), and every oriented triple determinant. Equality of the Gram matrices gives an isometry between the spans of the two vector collections. When their common span has rank 3, equality of oriented triple determinants forces orientation preservation. When rank ≤2, the isometry can be extended on its orthogonal complement to an element of SO(3) by choosing the remaining orientation. Since `SU(2)→SO(3)` by conjugation is onto, the vectors and hence all g_i are related by one common SU(2) conjugation. Tree-gauge reconstruction gives gauge equivalence of the original edge configurations. QED.

**Limitations.** The generator count grows with graph cycle rank, loop paths can be long, and finite orbit-separating coordinates provide **no quantitative spectral frame**. Length ≤3 means length in *cycle generators*, not three physical graph links.

## 3. Theorem ST-2: linear span of *single* Wilson traces is complete

Let W_γ(U)=`Tr_½ Hol_γ(U)` for **all** closed graph paths γ, allowing repeated or oppositely traversed edges, noncontractible/winding paths, and arbitrarily long words. Define `S_G=span_C{1,W_γ: γ closed}`.

**Statement.** `S_G` is uniformly dense in the continuous gauge-invariant functions `C(SU(2)^E/SU(2)^V)`. For any finite-volume Wilson Hamiltonian with positive electric coefficients and unique continuous strictly positive gauge-invariant vacuum Ω,

`closure span{Q_Ω W_γ Ω : γ closed}= Ω^⊥` within the physical Hilbert space.

**Proof.** By ST-1, the finitely many length ≤3 trace coordinates separate points on the compact gauge-orbit quotient. They are real, so Stone–Weierstrass gives density of the unital algebra they generate. For SU(2) matrices U,V, Cayley–Hamilton implies `V+V^(−1)=(Tr V)I`, hence

`(Tr U)(Tr V)=Tr(UV)+Tr(UV^(−1))`.

Transport any two closed holonomies to a common base point along graph paths; their traces are unchanged. The right-hand terms are again traces of single closed paths. Induction reduces every trace polynomial to a finite **linear combination** of single Wilson traces, so `S_G` contains the dense trace algebra. The positive vacuum identifies multiplication by Ω as a unitary `L²(Ω²dU)→H_phys`, and continuous invariant functions are dense in this L². Projecting off Ω yields the claimed physical cyclicity. QED.

**Qualification:** This is finite-graph Hilbert-space completeness, NOT a uniform quantitative low-energy observability bound and NOT continuum Hilbert-space reconstruction. Restricting paths to simple plaquettes, contractible loops, bounded length, or one symmetry channel invalidates the completeness claim.

## 4. Corollary ST-3: one common decay exponent for **all single traces** would give the gap

Let K_G=H_G−E0(G), or the corresponding normalized transfer with physical time a_t. Suppose there exists one `m>0`, independent of finite graph, cutoff and admitted boundaries, such that for **every** closed path γ of every such graph,

`C_γ(t)=<QW_γΩ,e^(−tK_G) QW_γΩ> ≤ M_{γ,G}e^(−mt)` for all t≥0,

where the source-dependent `M_{γ,G}<∞` is independent of t (no uniform amplitude bound in γ or G is required). Then `K_G≥mQ_Ω` on the physical Hilbert space for each graph.

**Proof.** For a positive source spectral measure μ_γ, if μ_γ([0,s])>0 for some s<m, then `C_γ(t)≥e^(−st)μ_γ([0,s])`, contradicting the asserted e^(−mt) bound as t→∞. Thus the spectral projection `P_(0,m)(K_G)` annihilates every `QW_γΩ`. ST-2 makes their span dense, so `P_(0,m)=0`. QED. This **removes a separate uniform frame hypothesis from the formulation** but is a *gap-strength conditional implication*, not new interacting decay.

## 5. Theorem CO-1: exact composite-mode obstruction (strictly positive transfer)

On four configurations `(x,y)∈{±1}²`, let `q(x,y)=xy` and define for `0<ρ<1` the symmetric Markov transfer matrix

`T_ρ((x,y),(x',y'))=1/4[1+ρ (xy)(x'y')]`.

Every entry is strictly positive, all row sums are one, and the eigenfunctions `1,x,y,xy` have eigenvalues `1,0,0,ρ` respectively. The unique normalized positive vacuum is constant. For all n≥1,

`C_x(n)=C_y(n)=0` but `C_xy(n)=ρ^n`.

As ρ↑1, the physical gap `−logρ/a_t` tends to zero although both individual coordinate sources have **perfect one-step decay**. Moreover the connected four-source cumulant of `(x_0,y_0,x_n,y_n)` is `ρ^n`; all cross-time pair cumulants of the individual coordinates vanish. **Proof:** direct summation over the four states yields the indicated eigenspaces; centered pair partitions vanish. QED.

This counterexample is an **abstract finite-state positive-transfer system**, NOT SU(2) Wilson gauge theory. It rules out the general inference 'a finite set of orbit-separating coordinate sources has rapid two-point decay, therefore all polynomial composite sources decay'. An additional SU(2)-specific dynamical theorem might still establish such a propagation, but cannot follow solely from positivity and coordinate completeness.

## 6. Earliest genuinely unresolved step: temporal connected multi-loop mixing

The historical program already knows complete Wilson algebra and exact source-inserted trace identities. ST-1/ST-2 sharpen the *choice of sources*. CO-1 shows why bounding only coordinate-generator correlators is inadequate. A noncircular intermediate research theorem, weaker than the desired full gap, is:

**Target CM-4 (OPEN):** for the actual interacting SU(2) Penrose transfer along the specified physical strong-magnetic trajectory, and for prescribed fixed local based loop words U,V,W,X, derive from the action and reflection-positive transfer an estimate

`|κ_4(U(0),V(0),W(t),X(t))| ≤ C_{U,V,W,X;h,G} exp(−m_4 t)`

with an explicitly derived positive rate m_4 independent of refinement and volume and prefactor independent of t. The cumulant must use *vacuum-connected, physically normalized source insertions*, not uninserted partition ratios or conditional estimator covariance. It must cover any boundary/center sector claimed. This is a **necessary first composite-source control**, not a sufficient proof of the full gap; all higher composite orders/word lengths and winding sectors would still require a compatible induction.

A valid certificate would specify complete oriented loop words and transfer action, derive a convergent source-inserted expansion or independently controlled block-crossing estimate including large fields, establish constants in physical time, and prove an induction controlling arbitrarily long single Wilson paths. Assumptions equivalent to spectral gap, universal high-order mixing, or an a priori complete frame are disallowed.

**Status:** ST-1, ST-2, ST-3 implication and CO-1 are independently proved; CM-4 and the complete source-inserted hierarchy remain OPEN. The project has **not** established a mass gap.
