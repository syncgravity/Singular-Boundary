# Branched positive-character kernels for SU(2) Wilson tree networks
27 October 2026. Exact restricted theorem; arbitrary Penrose prism interiors remain open.

Let G=SU(2), normalized Haar dg, d(g)=1-Tr(g)/2, q(b)=int_G exp(-b d(g))dg=e^-b 2I_1(b)/b. Denote K_b(g)=exp(-b d(g))/q(b). Let T be any finite rooted tree, root r with h_r=I, and a spin h_v in G at each other vertex. The normalized cavity law is product over oriented tree edges (u,v) of K_{b_e}(h_u^-1 h_v) dh_v. This is normalized for every size and topology: the edge increments are independent after a triangular Haar coordinate change.

At selected vertices v in Q add w_v(h_v)=exp(-gamma_v d(h_v)); multiple weights at one vertex permitted. Assume b_e>0 and gamma_v>=0; no condition relating these couplings. Then

R_Q:= E_tree prod_{v in Q} w_v(h_v) >= prod_{v in Q} q(gamma_v).
Therefore F_Q=-log R_Q <= sum_{v in Q}-log q(gamma_v). In particular for gamma_v in [c_-lambda,c_+lambda], lambda>=1, F_Q <= (3/2)|Q| log(lambda)+C|Q|, with constant depending only on c_+ (and c_- if desired), independent of tree diameter, branching number, number of other edges, and the boundary of the finite tree.

**Proof via positive-character messages.** A central continuous function f has positive character expansion if f(g)=sum_{j in {0,1/2,1,...}} a_j chi_j(g), a_j>=0, with absolutely convergent series. Its Haar mean is a_0. The Wilson weight w_gamma(g)=e^-gamma exp(gamma Tr(g)/2) has coefficients a_j=e^-gamma*(2j+1)*2 I_{2j+1}(gamma)/gamma >=0 (with gamma=0 by continuity), and a_0=q(gamma). Each K_b is central positive-character with normalized trivial coefficient 1 and positive Fourier multipliers alpha_j=I_{2j+1}(b)/I_1(b)>0. If f is positive-character, K_b*f is positive-character and has the same trivial coefficient a_0. If f,g are positive-character, fg is positive-character because SU(2) Clebsch-Gordan multiplication chi_j chi_k = sum_{l=|j-k|}^{j+k}chi_l (steps 1) has nonnegative integral coefficients, and (fg)_0 >= f_0 g_0. At each vertex the upward message is w_v times products of edge-convolved child messages. By induction the message is positive-character and its trivial coefficient >= product of q(gamma_v) in its descendant subtree. At the root, the terminal message is a positive-character function evaluated at I. Since chi_j(I)=2j+1>0, its value at identity is >= its Haar mean, which is >= prod q(gamma_v). QED.

This proof includes branching with paths from root to selected vertices overlapping many edges: no edge-disjointness hypothesis. It uses both noncommutative SU(2) convolution and fusion rules, not Gaussian approximation. It provides independent normalization and hence an exact jointly reweighted exterior endpoint law. Existence of a deterministic smooth joint transport follows by Moser's theorem on the compact product manifold for the two positive smooth Gibbs densities, but its spatial support and per-component action/Jacobian costs are not inferred. Combined insertion energy plus KL of the inserted law relative to cavity equals F_Q.

**A second theorem: boundary-message criterion.** If the rooted tree is coupled to a non-tree exterior via a nonnegative density/weight A(h_v), the above lower bound does not follow unless A admits a compatible positive-character tensor-network decomposition or another lower comparison. Pointwise conditional positivity of each message is insufficient: translated central character expansions can have signed coefficients. Hence this tree theorem cannot be applied to all faces of a three-dimensional Penrose prism complex by choosing an arbitrary link spanning tree: undeleted plaquette interactions become extra loop factors coupling several tree coordinates, violating the tree Gibbs factorization.

**Why this is not a full Penrose result.** Tree edge interactions are of principal-chiral/holonomy type K_b(h_u^-1h_v). A three-dimensional Yang-Mills plaquette factor contains ordered products of four link variables. A gauge-spanning tree of the link graph does not turn general plaquette interactions into independent tree-edge kernels. Multi-root closure interactions w(h_u^-1 h_v) with u,v not sharing a designated root can also break the simple central unary-message recursion. Spin-network contractions at cyclic vertices are not automatically nonnegative term-by-term. The positive-character message criterion must be verified at actual prism interfaces; this is not known for arbitrary Penrose cavity supports.

**Separate entropy/action warning.** Although E_nu V_Q+KL(nu||mu0)=F_Q <= sum -log q, it does not imply separately small expected change of retained action and negative log-Jacobian of an unspecified deterministic transport. A constructed transport must be audited for those individual terms.
