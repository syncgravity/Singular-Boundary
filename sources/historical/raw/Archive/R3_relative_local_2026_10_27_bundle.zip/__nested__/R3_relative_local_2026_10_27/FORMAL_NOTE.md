# R3 relative-local nonabelian gluing audit: exact one-edge flat collar, pointwise obstruction, and conditional compact-Haar theorem

**Date:** 2026-10-27. **Model:** constructed four-quadrilateral spatial disk crossed with a three-edge periodic time circle; 27 vertices, 63 edges, 48 faces, 12 prisms. **This is not a geometric Penrose extraction.** Source inputs are the previous `R3_nonabelian_relative_2026_10_27/prism_incidence.npz` and the exact Nielsen/Tietze presentation certificate. This continuation establishes relative **flat** gluing and conditional sufficient criteria, NOT a full arbitrary-support nonlinear R3 theorem.

## A. Relative eight-face support and an exact exterior-loop obstruction

Insert Q = [13,18,22,25,26,29,32,37]; retain 40 faces. Let L_0 be the union of edges of Q. It has 26 elements. The 37 remaining physical links are frozen exterior variables in the conditional partition function. From exact modular ranks, on L_0 the retained and all-face incidence matrices both have rank 25. On all 63 links their ranks are 35 and 36, so the exterior quadratic ranks are 10 (retained) and 11 (inserted). Hence an exterior rank-changing mode exists.

More strongly, the saved nonabelian retained-flat presentation is F(a,b) and the full-flat presentation is Z generated by b. Exhaustive free-group substitution using the previously certified Tietze moves constructs two **based, exterior-only graph cycles** C45, C53 whose words in the retained flat presentation are a b and b, respectively. Therefore the based exterior loop C45 C53^{-1} has word **a**, an exact nonabelian identity, and it uses *no link of L_0*. Choose a=-I, b=I and all retained relator coordinates identity. This is a retained flat SU(2) assignment with the frozen exterior loop equal to -I. Any fully flat assignment must have a=I, hence that exterior loop is I. Varying only the 26 links of L_0 can never make every plaquette flat.

By compactness of SU(2)^26, for this frozen exterior configuration xi_0, min_u S_full(u;xi_0)=lambda delta, where delta>0 is the minimum **unit-coupling** full action, whereas min_u S_ret(u;xi_0)=0. A local Haar ball of radius O(lambda^-1/2) around the retained flat minimizer gives Z_ret(xi_0)>=c lambda^-39 for lambda>=1. Conversely Z_full(xi_0)<=exp(-lambda delta), so

    Z_full(xi_0)/Z_ret(xi_0) <= c^-1 lambda^39 exp(-lambda delta).

**Thus no uniform polynomial-in-lambda conditional lower comparison can hold for the initial 26-link region, even though the unconditional insertion ratio for this finite model is known to be Theta(lambda^-3/2).** This pinpoints why averaging exterior holonomies or enlarging the repaired region is indispensable. The argument is exact; delta is not numerically evaluated here.

## B. Enlarging the collar by ONE edge eliminates the linear obstruction

For each exterior edge e, independently compute the two local ranks after replacing L_0 by L_0∪{e}. Four edges, **23, 43, 45, 53**, make the difference in exterior ranks zero. For e=45:

    local adjustable edges                  26 -> 27
    rank(D_ret restricted to local edges)  25 -> 25
    rank(D_all restricted to local edges)  25 -> 26
    exterior effective ranks (ret,all)   (10,11)->(10,10).

These ranks are verified again by independent exact Gaussian elimination mod prime 1000003, not inferred from a singular-value tolerance. Linear rank preservation after one collar link is a property of this finite support, not a general bounded-degree theorem.

## C. Exact nonabelian **flat-state gluing** relative to all 36 frozen links

Set L_1=L_0∪{45} and E_1=all other links. Restrict any retained flat assignment to the graph E_1. In the same two-generator description F(a,b), **every based loop contained entirely in E_1 has a word in b alone**: the exterior-loop carrying a used the removed edge 45. Therefore its SU(2) holonomies agree with those of the full-flat representation a↦I, b↦b.

Choose a spanning forest of the graph E_1. On each connected component, define vertex gauges by comparing the root-to-vertex parallel transports of the retained flat assignment and of the full-flat assignment. The edge identity

    U_old(e) = g_tail(e) U_full(e) g_head(e)^(-1)

holds **exactly as a reduced free-group word** on all 36 edges in E_1; this is checked edge by edge. Gauge-transform the full-flat assignment by these gauges. Its restriction to E_1 agrees with the original retained flat assignment exactly, and its holonomy around all 48 faces is the identity. Only five of the 27 adjustable edges change: [45,46,47,48,51]. The vertex gauge words have length at most one (either the identity or a). This construction works for **every** a,b∈SU(2), not just infinitesimally near the identity.

This is a genuinely relative, ambient-canceling statement **on the flat stratum** of the fixed model: the frozen exterior physical edge values remain unchanged. It is not an action-preserving map on arbitrary curved configurations and is not a measure-preserving map in a released holonomy, because setting a=I collapses an SU(2) coordinate.

A separate local Tietze audit with exterior symbols treated as frozen finds 25 eliminable retained face/link pairs, maximal substituted relator length 12 and pivot replacement length at most 5, with two local residual coordinates for the 27-link collar. Crucially, after these 25 eliminations all eight nontrivial residual retained relators are **words exclusively in frozen exterior symbols**, with neither surviving local generator occurring in them. This is an exact *flat-quotient* relative splitting. It does not imply that the retained Wilson action is independent of the two local free coordinates when the 25 eliminated relator holonomies are nontrivial. This bounds the *combinatorial* complexity for this particular finite example; it does not prove a Gibbs-density estimate near generic exterior fields.

## D. Why bounded incidence alone does NOT guarantee O(|Q|) local repair

For each n>=2, form a one-vertex 2-complex with n loop links x_0,...,x_(n-1), retained relators x_i x_(i+1)^-1 for 0<=i<n-1, and one inserted relator x_0. Every retained face has word length 2, the inserted face length 1, and each link is in at most two faces. The retained group is Z generated by a common holonomy a; adding x_0=1 kills it.

If the adjustable region is the first m<n links x_0,...,x_(m-1), while x_m,...,x_(n-1) are frozen, then the retained/full projected local incidence ranks are equal, so the extra exterior rank is 1. Only if **all n links** are adjustable does it fall to zero. Thus even one inserted face may require repair across an arbitrarily long chain in a bounded-degree 2-complex. This is an exact nonabelian group relation and is verified for n=4,8,16,32.

**Limitation:** this counterexample is NOT itself an edge-to-edge quadrilateral-prism manifold or Penrose prism complex; it disproves an inference from bounded degree/face length alone, not the desired Penrose theorem. The Penrose-prism geometry must supply an additional short-filling, local-cofibration, or bounded-collar hypothesis.

## E. Rigorous conditional compact-Haar normal-form criterion

Here is an actionable *sufficient theorem* for the actual nonlinear SU(2) Wilson measure, with the missing probabilistic obligation explicit.

For an insertion support Q, choose a local set of m <= A|Q| physical link coordinates (possibly with one-cell collar) and denote the exterior by xi. Let rho_0 be the exterior marginal of the cavity Gibbs law. Suppose there is an event G in exterior configuration space with rho_0(G)>=exp(-B|Q|). For each xi in G, assume a **Haar-preserving** coordinate chart of the local links into y∈SU(2)^k and a∈SU(2)^r, with k+r=m and k,r<=A|Q|, in which for some real E_0(xi)=min_u S_ret(u;xi), and constants 0<c<=C<infinity, A_0>=0 independent of ambient size and lambda:

    S_ret(y,a;xi)-E_0(xi) >= c lambda sum_i d(y_i),
    S_full(y,a;xi)-E_0(xi) <= A_0|Q| + C lambda [sum_i d(y_i)+sum_j d(a_j)]

for **all** local y,a, with d(g)=1-Tr(g)/2. A fixed spectator Haar coordinate independent of both actions may be included without affecting the result. The inequalities may also be required only for a Haar chart of comparable-volume good local configurations if their density and Jacobian are explicitly accounted for; that extension is NOT invoked here.

Then by exact product Haar and q(t)=2e^-t I_1(t)/t, the conditional ratio satisfies for each xi∈G:

    Z_full(xi)/Z_ret(xi) >= exp(-A_0|Q|) q(C lambda)^(k+r) / q(c lambda)^k.

Averaging against rho_0, this gives the rigorous **ambient-uniform** conclusion

    Z_full/Z_ret >= exp(-(A_0+B)|Q|) q(C lambda)^(k+r) / q(c lambda)^k.

Since q(t)=Theta((1+t)^(-3/2)) with global constants, the resulting free energy obeys

    -log(Z_full/Z_ret) <= (3/2)r log(1+lambda) + O(|Q|)

with constants independent of the ambient complex. This is a theorem under the stated chart and exterior-event hypotheses, **not** an existence theorem for such charts or events on arbitrary Penrose prism supports.

For the previously verified global Nielsen chart of the *isolated* eight-face complex, k=35, r=1, c=1/342, C=609, A_0=B=0, and the theorem reduces to the previously established finite-complex Haar ratio bound. However, this chart changes coordinates throughout that finite complex; the relative 27-link flat gluing alone does **not** verify the stronger curved-field inequalities.

## F. Required new input before claiming arbitrary Penrose R3

1. **Relative nonabelian collar certificate on actual Penrose prism complexes.** For each Q, choose a collar with <=A|Q| links and prove its retained/full flat restriction admits a short nonlinear retraction with uniformly bounded word lengths/overlap. The constructed example needs just one collar edge, but the chain counterexample shows bounded degree alone cannot prove this.
2. **Quantitative off-flat normal form.** Prove Haar-Jacobian control and nonlinear action comparisons (as in Section E), uniform in the ambient cavity, *for a sufficiently probable set of exterior configurations*. A flat-only map does not control positive Wilson weights on generic curved configurations.
3. **Exterior good-event estimate.** Show rho_0(G)>=exp(-B|Q|) or an equivalent relative-entropy / correlated-transport comparison for the actual retained Wilson Gibbs measure. This is the missing measure-theoretic step. There is no basis for claiming that Penrose bounded local complexity establishes it automatically.
4. **Continuum regime.** Check these bounds for couplings lambda→∞ along an actual discretization/renormalization trajectory, with geometry provenance and physical metric specifications. A lattice insertion bound alone does not prove a continuum mass gap.

The result of this stage is an exact **one-edge relative nonabelian flat gluing** and a mathematically explicit **conditional compact-Haar completion criterion**, together with a sharp obstruction to asserting a pointwise local result without a collar or exterior averaging. The fully general nonlinear R3 theorem remains OPEN.
