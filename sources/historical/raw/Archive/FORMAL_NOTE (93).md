# Nonabelian relative-face presentation and exact compact-Haar gluing comparison
**Date:** 2026-10-27  
**Model:** constructed four-quadrilateral spatial disk × three-edge periodic time circle, with SU(2) Wilson action. **Not** a geometrically certified Penrose-prism extraction.  
**Support:** `Q=[13,18,22,25,26,29,32,37]` (eight temporal faces).  
**Status:** exact nonabelian presentation, exact Haar coordinate change, rigorously verified finite-complex two-sided nonlinear insertion bound; no ambient-independent theorem for arbitrary Penrose supports.

## 1. Exact group presentation

The cellular data (`prism_incidence.npz`) give 27 vertices, 63 edges and 48 faces; the 26-link spanning tree is certified in `presentation.json`. Gauge-fixing along the tree leaves 37 independent, normalized Haar SU(2) chords. The 40 retained face words and eight inserted words are reconstructed from the **oriented** link/face incidence, not only the abelianized boundary matrix.

35 explicitly recorded elementary Tietze eliminations remove 35 retained relators and their generators. The remaining five retained relators freely reduce to the identity, leaving the nonabelian presentation

    pi_1(retained 2-complex) = F(a,b) (rank-two free group).

In the saved chord ordering, `a` is generator 19 (physical edge 26), and `b` is generator 34 (physical edge 59). Under the retained relations, the inserted face words reduce in face-ID order to

    face 13: 1; 18:1; 22:1; 25:a^-1; 26:a^-1; 29:a^-1; 32:a^-1; 37:1.

Therefore the full flat complex has `pi_1(full)=F(a,b)/<<a>>=Z` generated by `b`. This proves that one additional *nonabelian* SU(2) flat holonomy is released by deletion of Q; the rank difference alone had not proved this.

## 2. Exact normalized Haar coordinates, and why they do not factor the Gibbs measure

At each elementary Tietze step, solve an oriented cyclic face word `x^s tail` for the eliminated chord `x`, but **retain a new independent group coordinate** `y_f` rather than imposing the face relation:

- `x = y_f tail^-1` if s=+1;
- `x = tail y_f^-1` if s=-1.

Here `tail` is a word only in other currently active group coordinates. Both changes preserve normalized Haar measure, conditional on those other coordinates (left/right translation and inversion). After 35 such transformations the original product-Haar measure is exactly

    product_{f in selected retained faces} dy_f  · da · db.

No quadratic regulator or Jacobian approximation is involved. `audit_haar.py` tests all 48 original face holonomies against their transformed words at 100 noncommuting SU(2) assignments; maximum quaternion residual is <1e-15.

But the **retained Wilson action does not factor** away from the flat stratum. Eight retained face words contain `a` and ten contain `b` in this coordinate system. In particular retained face 9 is the exact group word

    y_28^-1 · y_9 · a · y_30 · a^-1 · y_8^-1

(where the `y_f` numbering comes from generator label `38+f`). Set `y_9=y_30=i sigma_x`, all other `y_f=I`, `b=I`, and `a=exp(i theta sigma_z)`; then

    (1/2) Tr W_9 = -cos(2 theta).

So changing the liberated Haar coordinate `a` changes an actual retained Wilson plaquette by an O(1) amount; at coupling lambda the retained action may change by O(lambda). This excludes the **naive** claim that this particular Haar-coordinate choice gives an independent Haar factor in the retained Gibbs law. It does NOT exclude every possible alternative nonlinear transport preserving the retained action.

## 3. Global, exact SU(2) Wilson-action coercivity from triangular relators

Write `d(g)=1-(1/2)Re Tr(g)`, and `delta(g)=sqrt(d(g))=||g-I||_F/2`. The normalized Frobenius chord distance satisfies

    delta(uv) <= delta(u)+delta(v),
    delta(h u h^-1)=delta(u),
    delta(u^-1)=delta(u).

In the transformed 37-variable coordinates, the selected retained relators are **triangular**: when the earlier newly introduced `y` variables are set to identity, the cyclically reduced `j`th selected retained face word is precisely `y_j` or `y_j^-1`. The exact certificate verifies all 35 steps. Let `s_j=delta(y_j)` and `r_j=delta(W_{f_j})`. If `L[j,i]` counts occurrences of an earlier `y_i` in this word, then

    s_j <= r_j + sum_{i<j} L[j,i] s_i.

Thus `s <= M r` componentwise with the **finite nonnegative integer** matrix `M=(I-L)^-1`. `quantitative_certificate.json` gives `||M||_1=19`, `||M||_infty=18` for the retained action; therefore `||M||_2^2<=342`, and

    (1/342) sum_j d(y_j) <= sum_{p in R} d(W_p).

Conversely, deleting all y letters from each retained word leaves the identity, for *every* a,b. The chord-distance triangle inequality gives an upper comparison, where the audited sum of squared active-letter occurrences is 360:

    sum_{p in R} d(W_p) <= 360 sum_j d(y_j).

For the full action, choose the same 35 retained relators followed by inserted face 25, whose residual word is `a^-1`. This is a 36-step triangular presentation relative to the remaining free generator b. The exact nonnegative recurrence has `||M_full||_1=21`, `||M_full||_infty=18`, and the full-word upper count is 609. Therefore

    (1/378) [sum_j d(y_j)+d(a)]
        <= sum_{p in R union Q} d(W_p)
        <= 609 [sum_j d(y_j)+d(a)].

These are **global inequalities for all SU(2)^37 configurations**, not Gaussian approximations and not just infinitesimal near a flat connection. They are conservative but explicitly machine-checkable finite constants. The proof is valid because every required cyclic reduction is a free-group identity; the numerical computations only audit that combinatorial certificate.

## 4. Exact, all-coupling compact-Haar insertion sandwich

For equal Wilson coupling lambda on all 48 faces define

    Z_R(lambda)=∫ exp[-lambda sum_{p in R} d(W_p)] product_e dU_e,
    Z_all(lambda)=∫ exp[-lambda sum_{p=0}^{47} d(W_p)] product_e dU_e.

The tree gauge fixation and the 35 Nielsen changes preserve Haar exactly. Let

    q(t)=∫_SU2 exp[-t d(g)] dg = 2 exp(-t) I_1(t)/t,  q(0)=1.

Applying the *global* action inequalities and integrating independent Haar coordinates gives **for every lambda>0**:

    Z_R(lambda) <= q(lambda/342)^35,
    Z_R(lambda) >= q(360 lambda)^35,
    Z_all(lambda) >= q(609 lambda)^36,
    Z_all(lambda) <= q(lambda/378)^36.

Consequently,

    q(609 lambda)^36 / q(lambda/342)^35
       <= Z_all(lambda)/Z_R(lambda)
       <= q(lambda/378)^36 / q(360 lambda)^35.

As q(t)~sqrt(2/pi)t^-3/2 for t→∞, **rigorously**

    Z_all(lambda)/Z_R(lambda) = Theta(lambda^-3/2)

for this finite constructed 3-step eight-face cavity. Thus

    -log(Z_all/Z_R) = (3/2) log(lambda) + O(1)

with a finite geometry-dependent additive constant. The cost depends on *one* independent compact SU(2) flat holonomy, not eight independently constrained plaquettes. This proves a support-logarithmic nonlinear insertion estimate for this **fixed example**, without Gaussian regularization. The explicit constants are extremely loose; they do not by themselves give an ambient-independent bound for growing complexes.

For face couplings between c_-lambda and c_+lambda (positive fixed c_±), the same argument applies after multiplying the lower-action constants by c_- and the upper-action constants by c_+.

## 5. A clean-flat Laplace cross-check (not needed for the bound)

The exact flat sets are `y_f=I` with (a,b) arbitrary for R, and `y_f=a=I` with b arbitrary for all 48 faces. The selected retained face map has an invertible 105×105 normal differential at every flat R connection (the triangular group coordinates ensure this), while selected 35 faces plus face25 have an invertible 108×108 differential at every fully flat connection. Explicit quaternion-adjoint Jacobian tests, including identity, center and noncommuting free holonomies, give log absolute determinant 0 to roundoff and positive minimum singular values. The compact clean-minimum Laplace method therefore also yields an asymptotic **positive finite** coefficient `Z_all/Z_R ~ c_* lambda^-3/2`, rather than only two-sided bounds.

The optional 256-point Haar quadrature of normal Hessian determinants estimates `c_* ≈ 3.10e-8`, with no certified numerical error; the exact exponent does **not** depend on that Monte Carlo estimate.

## 6. What this does NOT prove for arbitrary Penrose supports

- The released `a` is a Haar coordinate under the *unweighted* compact measure, **not independent of the retained Gibbs weight** at generic field configurations. Exact factorization into q of this one variable is unjustified.
- The coercivity and word-length constants `(342,360,378,609)` come from a complete 37-variable Nielsen presentation of this **fixed complex**. They are not shown to remain bounded, or to cancel on exterior coordinates, for arbitrarily large Penrose cavities.
- A support-local theorem for arbitrary Penrose prism Q requires a **relative** nonabelian Nielsen/gluing certificate with (i) O(|Q|) repaired coordinates, (ii) uniformly bounded word lengths and overlap, and (iii) exterior retained Gibbs factors cancelling or having a uniformly controlled Radon–Nikodym loss. The general existence of such certificates is OPEN.
- The arbitrary finite-complex linear rank increment by itself is insufficient: a nontrivial nonabelian relator can prevent a primitive free Haar coordinate.
- This does not prove a Yang–Mills continuum mass gap, a claim about the 3D geometry of a Penrose extraction, or a universal nonlinear R3 estimate.

The bundled `prism_incidence.npz` makes these scripts self-contained, requiring numpy (and scipy only for optional q evaluations, not the theorem proof).
