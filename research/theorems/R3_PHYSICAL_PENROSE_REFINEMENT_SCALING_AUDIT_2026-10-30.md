# Physical coefficient scaling versus two-link center-cap criterion (2026-10-30)

Scope: audit of the EXISTING finite SU2 Penrose-disk model against a conventional Kogut-Susskind continuum scaling. No claim that a particular scaling convention was already fixed in repository.

On an isotropic regular spatial lattice in d dimensions, a standard Wilson Hamiltonian has electric coefficient a(h)=g_d^2/(2 h^(d-2)) and magnetic coefficient b(h)=C_d/(g_d^2 h^(4-d)), with positive dimensionless C_d depending on the convention p=Tr(U)/2 and magnetic subtraction. Therefore b/a=2 C_d h^(2*d-6)/g_d^4. For d=2 at fixed dimensionful g_2^2, a=g_2^2/2 and b~1/(g_2^2 h^2), hence b/a~1/(g_2^4 h^2), diverging as h->0. For d=3 at fixed bare g_3^2, a=g_3^2/(2h), b~1/(g_3^2 h), ratio is order 1/g_3^4; a continuum limit demands the appropriate coupling running, not an assumption of vanishing b/a. These formulas describe regular-lattice reference conventions, NOT a derivation of the uniquely appropriate nonuniform Penrose weights.

For the fixed finite disk two-link theorem, gamma_loc=(3/4) min(a_e,a_d), eps=b_g/(gamma_loc-b_g), and cap Haar mass c_delta~delta^(3/2). The elementary cap inequality is useful at delta~h only if eps=O(h^(3/4)), so the sufficient weak-magnetic requirement is b_g/min(a_e,a_d)=O(h^(3/4)) with a suitably small constant. Standard d=2 fixed-g scaling violates this sufficient condition strongly; d=3 fixed bare g also does not satisfy it. This failure is a limitation of the estimate, not evidence that physical center cap probability vanishes.

Crucially, a two-dimensional spatial Penrose disk defines a natural 2+1 spacetime Hamiltonian regulator. A 3+1 spacetime mass-gap claim requires an explicit THREE-dimensional spatial lattice/fabric and its gauge Hamiltonian or another mathematically rigorous dimensional reduction. Neither is provided by the eleven-face disk. Open tasks: (1) explicitly choose spatial dimension and target continuum theory; (2) derive Penrose edge electric and face magnetic weights from geometric dual volumes, units and bare coupling; (3) show stability under refinement; (4) prove a nonperturbative local cap comparison in the regime b/a not small. No refinement-uniform center cap or continuum Yang-Mills mass gap is established.
