# R3-IR — All-orders connected-spectator cancellation and factorial gate

**Finite planar original-link SU(2) theorem; 2026-10-31.** For the vacuum connected covariance of two Wilson observables under independently coupled retained plaquettes, any multivariate Taylor coefficient with an active plaquette component disconnected (in the original-link incidence graph) from the component containing both observables **vanishes exactly**. If no incidence component contains both observables, every connected-covariance coefficient also vanishes. Proof: turn off couplings absent from the coefficient; the original-link Hamiltonian factorizes by incidence component, its unique positive ground state is the tensor product, and the normalized connected expectation is independent of all spectator couplings. This strengthens the earlier theorem that only excluded coefficients when no active component linked the observables.

**Time-ordering warning:** For delta>0, the ordered integral ∫_(0<t1<...<tn<∞) exp(-delta tn) dt1...dtn=delta^(-n), by integrating the (n−1)-simplex t_n^(n−1)/(n−1)!. Bounding n! independent insertion orders separately therefore gives n! delta^(-n), not an automatic factorial gain. This observation does not establish factorial growth of *actual* Wilson connected coefficients; further linked-cumulant cancellation remains necessary.
