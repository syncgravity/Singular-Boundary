# Seven-face squarefree ground-state response has no energy counterterms
Date: 2026-10-30. Proven at fixed regulator for the periodic M=5 seven-active-face Hamiltonian; actual coefficient nonvanishing OPEN.

Set S={1,2,3,4,7,8,10}, H(b)=T-sum_(g in S)b_g p_g, dropping the scalar sum b_g from the conventional nonnegative Wilson Hamiltonian. T is the positive product-link SU(2) electric Laplacian restricted to gauge invariants; its zero mode is the normalized constant 1, and R=T_perp^-1 denotes its zero-mean inverse. Let normalized positive ground state Omega(b), eigenvalue eps(b), with eps(0)=0. The periodic M5 edge incidence is reconstructed by penrose_causal_study.Approximant(M=5), wilson_torus.penrose_faces. Each active face has an edge appearing in NO other active face: face 1 edge 0, face 2 edge 88, face 3 edge 87, face 4 edge 372, face 7 edge 25, face 8 edge 9, face 10 edge 13. (Each edge still belongs to another face in the full torus, but its coupling is inactive.) Therefore central sign flip on one such edge reverses exactly one of the seven magnetic couplings and commutes with T. It preserves gauge invariance. Thus eps(b) is separately EVEN in each b_g, and all squarefree positive-degree coefficients of eps vanish.

Expand the eigenfunction in a formal convergent multivariable Taylor series in sufficiently small b, choosing intermediate normalization <1,Psi(b)>=1, which is locally valid (the L2 normalized Omega differs by a scalar). With coefficients Psi_alpha and eps_alpha, recurrence for alpha !=0:
Psi_alpha=R[sum_(g:alpha_g>0) p_g Psi_(alpha-e_g) + sum_(0<beta<=alpha) eps_beta Psi_(alpha-beta)] with sign convention H=T-sum b p, and eps_alpha=-sum_g <1,p_g Psi_(alpha-e_g)>.
For any squarefree multiindex alpha, eps_beta=0 for all nonempty beta<=alpha because eps is even in every coupling. Hence Psi_alpha =R sum_(g in support alpha) p_g Psi_(alpha-e_g), yielding for alpha=(1,...,1) the EXACT 7! term ordered sum of resolvent chains
Psi_(1,...,1)=sum_(sigma in S7) R p_sigma1 R p_sigma2 ... R p_sigma7 1.
Furthermore the L2 normalization scalar of Omega(b) is separately even in each coupling (unitary link sign flips and uniqueness), so its squarefree nonconstant coefficients vanish; consequently the same squarefree coefficient applies to Q_f Omega(b). This resolves the energy-counterterm issue raised in R3_M5_SEVEN_FACE_PARITY_COMPATIBLE_PROBE_2026-10-30.md: the previously introduced 5040-chain amplitude is indeed the EXACT squarefree coefficient paired with the probe O=(p_f^2-1/4)prod_(g in S)p_g.

The statement concerns a truncated finite periodic torus seven-active-face model, not the fully coupled Penrose lattice, not the open disk and not a continuum mass gap.
