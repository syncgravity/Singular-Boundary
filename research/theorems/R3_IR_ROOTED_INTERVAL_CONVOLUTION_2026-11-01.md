# Rooted interval convolution bound

For nonnegative interval activities a_I,b_J with |I|≤j, |J|≤k on any finite open straight chain and ρ≥0, set A_j(ρ)=sup_x Σ_{I∋x}exp(ρ(|I|−1))a_I, and similarly A_k. For touching pairs I,J (overlapping or adjacent), define B_{j,k}(ρ)=sup_x Σ_{I~J, x∈I∪J}exp(ρ(|I∪J|−1))a_Ib_J. Then B_{j,k}(ρ)≤exp(ρ)(j+k+4)A_j(ρ)A_k(ρ), uniformly in chain length. Proof: union weight costs exp(ρ), and if x∈I then any J touching I intersects its one-site extension, yielding ≤(|I|+2)A_k≤(j+2)A_k; reverse roles for x∈J. Exact rational regression verifies 179 nonuniform finite cases. This closes the raw touching-interval counting step only; connected source identification and L² Poisson/connected-projection control remain open. It does not establish a physical Gevrey bound, interacting λ=1 gap, or continuum mass gap. See the accompanying complete verifier ZIP in Proof Audits.
