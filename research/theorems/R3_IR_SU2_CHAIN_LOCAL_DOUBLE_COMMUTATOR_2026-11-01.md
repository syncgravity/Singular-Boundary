# Original-link SU(2) local double-commutator audit

The SU(2) Casimir normalization implies |∇_e p_f|²=(1−p_f²)/4 per boundary link, so [p_f,[K,p_f]]=2(1−p_f²), operator norm ≤2. Adjacent plaquettes share one link, with off-diagonal operator norm ≤1/2; nonadjacent entries vanish. Thus sup_f Σ_g ||[p_f,[K,p_g]]||≤3 independently of m.
