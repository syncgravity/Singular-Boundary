Original-link SU(2) dimension-free coordinate strong electric Poisson inverse. For any finite product SU(2)^E, K=Σ_e(−Δ_e) with fundamental Casimir 3/4, every bounded Haar-centered F has u=K^{-1}F satisfying **||∇_e u||∞ ≤ π||F||∞** for each individual original link e, independent of |E|. Proof uses factorized heat semigroup, monotonicity of SU(2) one-link radial heat kernel, and exact integrated L1 heat-kernel gradient: ∫₀∞||∇h_t||₁dt=(4/π)∫₀^πr sin²r dr=π. The one-link Green kernel derivative is G'(θ)=−4∫_θ^πsin²r dr/sin²θ in the radius-2 S³ metric. The heat-kernel monotonicity follows from parabolic maximum principle on radial derivatives. This does not bound summed derivatives uniformly over support, establish nonlinear gradient product closure, explicit λ=1 chain gap, signed current, or continuum Yang–Mills gap.
