# Exact mixed-Wilson-trace electric source and its algebraic scope
Date: 2026-10-30
Status: proved finite-volume chain-rule identity on retained-trace cylinder algebra; extension to the actual projected interacting vacuum OPEN.

On a finite SU(2) link lattice with T=sum_e a_e(-Delta_e), physical -Delta chi_(1/2)=(3/4)chi_(1/2), define p_g=(1/2)Tr W_g for each simple plaquette g and A_g=sum_{e in boundary g}a_e. The positive carré du champ Gamma_T(F,G)=sum_e a_e <grad_e F,grad_e G> satisfies:
T p_g=(3/4)A_g p_g;
Gamma_T(p_g,p_g)=(A_g/4)(1-p_g²);
Gamma_T(p_g,p_k)=0 when distinct plaquettes share no primal edge, as their trace variables use disjoint link sets. For a smooth retained-trace cylinder F=F(p_(g1),...,p_(gm)) with all gi != deleted face f, diffusion chain rule for positive T yields
T F = sum_i (3 A_i/4) p_i partial_i F -sum_i (A_i/4)(1-p_i²)partial_ii F -2 sum_(i<j)Gamma_T(p_i,p_j)partial_ij F.
The canonical deleted-face Haar projector P_f fixes all other plaquette words and traces; Q_f=I-P_f kills the first two sums. Therefore
-Q_f T F =2 sum_(i<j) (Q_f Gamma_T(p_i,p_j)) partial_ij F.
Here the partial derivatives depend only on retained traces and commute with P_f. The cross terms occur only at shared primal edges; the expression does not carry an explicit full dual-path sum. For F additive across individual p_g, all mixed partial derivatives vanish, and Q_f T F=0 exactly.

CAUTION: this formula applies to functions of individual retained plaquette TRACES. The actual p=P_f Omega is a general gauge-invariant function of jointly based retained holonomies, including joint orientation / spin-network invariants not generated by the separate traces. For such functions the complete transformed electric operator can contain additional mixed derivatives and connection coefficients. No assertion that its Q source is local or small is justified. Even for trace cylinders Q_f Gamma_T(p_g,p_k) need not be small or nonzero, and no refinement-uniform estimate is supplied.

This identifies an exactly source-free retained additive trace class and reduces the trace-cylinder source to nearest-neighbor mixed interactions. It does NOT establish the Penrose identity-cap lower bound, magnetic deletion scaling, or continuum mass gap.
