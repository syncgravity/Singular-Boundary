# Exact original-link SU(2) multilinear analytic electric inverse

On r consecutive face holonomies, the 4^r-dimensional space V_r of polynomials linear in each independent unit quaternion is invariant under the physical original-link electric operator. In tensor-coordinate coefficients, K_r=3r I+2Σ_{i=1}^{r−1}Σ_{k=1}³R_i^k L_(i+1)^k. Each R,L is a signed permutation divided by 2, giving ||K_r−3rI||_{1→1}≤(3/2)(r−1). Neumann inversion gives **||K_r⁻¹||_{1→1}≤2/[3(r+1)]**, valid for all r and with no loss in the fixed-degree coordinate analytic norm e^(σr)||c||₁. Exact SymPy quaternion matrices verify the physical cross-term norm 3/2,3,9/2 for r=2,3,4 (dimensions 16,64,256). The theorem holds for arbitrary r. It does not provide a corresponding inverse bound for general polynomial degree or across quaternion-sphere quotient representatives; a full analytic connected recurrence and interacting λ=1 gap remain unproved.
