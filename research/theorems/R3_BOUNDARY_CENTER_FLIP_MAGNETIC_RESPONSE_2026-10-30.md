# Free-boundary center-flip symmetry and strict local magnetic response (R3-IR-509)

Date: 2026-10-30. Let H(b)=H_E+V_rest-b p_f on a finite connected open SU(2) graph; p_f=Tr(U_f)/2. Assume a link e occurs exactly once in f and in NO other retained magnetic plaquette. Let Z_e act by multiplying U_e by -I; this is a measure-preserving unitary involution. For the specified positive inverse-Whitney kinetic form H_E=(g2²/2)D*KD, transports enter only through Ad of Wilson lines, and Ad(-U)=Ad(U). Left and right invariant link derivatives commute with Z_e. Therefore Z_e H_E Z_e=H_E, Z_e V_rest Z_e=V_rest and Z_e p_f Z_e=-p_f. Hence **Z_e H(b) Z_e=H(-b)** and E(b)=E(-b).

By Rayleigh-Ritz, E(b)=inf_{||psi||=1}<psi,H(0)psi>-b<psi,p_f psi> is concave. Evenness forces E(b) to be nonincreasing on b>=0. If the elliptic finite-link operator has a unique strictly positive ground state, the eigenvalue is analytic in b, and Hellmann-Feynman gives m(b)=<p_f>_b=-E'(b)>=0 for b>=0. Moreover E''(b)=-2 <(p_f-m)Omega,(H(b)-E(b))_perp^{-1}(p_f-m)Omega><0, since multiplication by the nonconstant plaquette p_f cannot leave a strictly positive Omega proportional to itself. Thus m(0)=0 and **m(b)>0 for every b>0**. The local susceptibility chi_f(b)=-E''(b)>0 and m(b)=integral_0^b chi_f(t)dt.

This is a genuine sign-and-strictness theorem for the *mean* fundamental plaquette, with arbitrary other bounded magnetic couplings. It requires the free-link center flip to preserve the rest of the Hamiltonian, and does not apply to an interior plaquette lacking such a link without another symmetry argument. It supplies NO positive environment-uniform lower bound on m(b) because chi_f can be arbitrarily small; m>0 does not imply <1-p_f²> small, and cannot yield a shrinking double-center cap. In fact, because p_f is continuous, no positive lower bound on its expectation automatically controls second moments at the center.
