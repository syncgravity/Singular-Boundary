# PF-I16 — Connected-support counting with source-uniform incidence constants

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Scope:** authentic five-direction coupled-\(\mathbb Z^5\) Penrose–Fibonacci accepted-rhombus complex.  
**Status:** universal combinatorial upper bound established from the elementary five-direction inventory; sharper degree-four bound is finite-crop evidence only. No all-orders quantum activity bound or interacting gap is claimed.

## 1. Incidence qualification

Two levels of incidence information must not be conflated.

* The elementary five-direction inventory gives the universal safe bound \(d_e\le 2(5-1)=8\) accepted coordinate-rhombi incident to an original link.
* The eight recorded original source crops \(N_{grid}=7,10,12,15,18,20,23,25\) have no edge with more than two accepted-face incidences. This is finite evidence, not a theorem for every accepted source region or refinement.

Accordingly, source-volume-uniform counting uses eight unless the strict triangular acceptance rule is separately proved to reduce the incidence maximum to two everywhere.

## 2. Face-overlap degree

Let \(\mathcal G_F\) have accepted rhombi as vertices, with adjacency when two faces share an original link. Each face has four boundary links. Each link belongs to at most eight candidate coordinate rhombi, hence
\[
\boxed{\Delta(\mathcal G_F)\le4(8-1)=28.}
\]
For the eight tested crops only, the observed maximum incidence is at most two and the crop-specific overlap degree is at most four.

## 3. Rooted connected-support count

For a connected set \(S\) of \(k\) faces containing a fixed root \(f\), choose a deterministic spanning tree and its depth-first closed traversal. It has length \(2(k-1)\); the visited-vertex set recovers \(S\). Thus
\[
\#\{S\ni f:|S|=k,\ S\text{ connected}\}\le D^{2(k-1)}.
\]
With the universal \(D=28\),
\[
\boxed{N_f(k)\le784^{k-1}.}
\]
The sharper \(16^{k-1}\) is valid on the eight tested crops, or on any future family for which the degree-four incidence claim is proved uniformly. It is not currently the universal source-volume constant.

## 4. Conditional cluster-summability implication

If a separate quantum theorem proves a volume-independent exact-support activity bound
\[
|\zeta_G(S;\mathbf b)|\le A q^{|S|},
\]
with disconnected supports vanishing by tensor factorization, then
\[
\sum_{S\ni f,\ S\text{ connected}}|\zeta_G(S;\mathbf b)|
\le A\sum_{k\ge1}784^{k-1}q^k
=\boxed{\frac{Aq}{1-784q}},\qquad784q<1.
\]
Thus the safe combinatorial threshold is \(q<1/784\); a uniformly proved degree-four incidence theorem would improve it to \(q<1/16\). This is a counting implication, not a proof of the activity estimate.

## 5. Existing Cauchy envelope does not close the gap

The fixed-support Riesz/Cauchy estimate gives, for a connected support of size \(k\) and first allowed multi-index of total degree \(2k\),
\[
|C_{(2,\ldots,2)}(S)|\le\frac{3a_0}{4}\left(\frac{4k}{3a_0}\right)^{2k}.
\]
Its \(k^{2k}\) growth is not absorbed by either exponential support count at fixed nonzero coupling. This shows that this Cauchy estimate plus connected-support counting is insufficient; it does not show that the true Wilson-vacuum expansion diverges.

The next quantum step must improve coefficients before absolute summation, for example by exact single-face dressing and a linked-cluster/Kirkwood–Thomas argument that bounds genuinely shared-link fusion while retaining original-link SU(2) recoupling and the infinite Peter–Weyl tower.
