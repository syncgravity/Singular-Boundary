Uniform small-coupling original-link SU(2) square-chain spectral gap. For H_m(lambda)=sum_e(-Delta_e)-lambda sum_f p_f on the full original-link tensor Hilbert space, each link is L²(SU2) with unique on-site vacuum and Casimir gap g=3/4. Each plaquette p_f is bounded in norm by 1 and supported within radius R=2 about a distinct anchor on the embedded Z² original-link midpoint lattice. Thus the interaction norm per anchor is ≤|lambda| and remains independent of chain length m. By Yarotsky's weak-interaction spectral gap stability theorem (J Math Phys 45 (2004) 2134, DOI 10.1063/1.1705718), there exist positive lambda_star and gamma_star, independent of m, such that for all |lambda|<lambda_star the full-Hilbert gap is at least gamma_star. Uniqueness and vertex SU2 symmetry imply the ground state is gauge invariant, so the physical sector also has at least that gap. No numerical threshold and no claim at lambda=1 or continuum scaling.
