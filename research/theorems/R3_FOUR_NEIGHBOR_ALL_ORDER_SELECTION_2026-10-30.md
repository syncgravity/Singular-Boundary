# Four-neighbor deleted-face selection rule and indexed periodic Penrose star
Date: 2026-10-30
Status: PROVED all-orders coupling-support selection rule, and explicit indexed FIVE-FACE STAR in a supplied PERIODIC Penrose approximant; nonzero fourth-order coefficient on an open Penrose disk remains OPEN.

Let f be a simple quadrilateral face in an edge-to-edge planar rhombus disk with four distinct edge neighbors g1,g2,g3,g4, with retained plaquette couplings b1,...,b4. All other retained couplings are arbitrary nonnegative and fixed. For H_f^-=T+sum_(g!=f)b_g(1-p_g), T=sum_e a_e(-Delta_e) with a_e>0, set q_f(b)=Q_f Omega_f^-(b), where Q_f is deleted-holonomy conditional Haar complement and Omega_f^- is unique normalized positive physical ground state.

If b_i=0 for any i, the shared edge e_i of f and g_i occurs in no remaining active Wilson potential: its only incident faces are f (deleted) and g_i (zero coupling). The complete product-link Hamiltonian separates a_ei(-Delta_ei) from the remaining links; the unique positive ground state is constant in U_ei and its deleted face holonomy is exactly Haar. Hence q_f(b)=0 on each coordinate hyperplane b_i=0. At any fixed finite regulator the ground state depends real-analytically on bounded coupling parameters near b=0 (and locally near any point with simple eigenvalue and consistent positive normalization). Analytic divisibility in four independent variables implies
q_f(b1,b2,b3,b4)=b1*b2*b3*b4 R_f(b1,b2,b3,b4)
for a local analytic L2-vector-valued R_f. Consequently all Taylor coefficients lacking any of the four neighbor couplings vanish. The FIRST POSSIBLE nonzero joint coefficient is fourth order; no proof that it is nonzero.
