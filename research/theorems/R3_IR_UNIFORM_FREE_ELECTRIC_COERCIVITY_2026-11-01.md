# Uniform free-electric coercivity and all-order L1 Riccati-source bound

For an open straight SU(2) original-link chain of m plaquettes, let D_m be the sum of independent SU(2) face Casimirs. Shared-link opposite-orientation electric derivatives and the scalar tridiagonal path matrix imply the rigorous quadratic-form comparison (4−2cos(π/(m+1)))D_m ≤ K_m ≤ (4+2cos(π/(m+1)))D_m. Since the product-Haar SU(2) Casimir Poincaré constant is 3/4, gap(K_m)≥(3/4)(4−2cos(π/(m+1)))≥3/2 uniformly in m; hence the mean-zero electric inverse has L2 norm at most 2/3. For polynomial score coefficients S_j of quaternion-coordinate degree ≤j, the Casimir upper bound j(j+2)/4 and carré-du-champ Cauchy–Schwarz give ||F_n||_1 ≤ (3/4)Σ_{j=1}^{n−1}sqrt(j(j+2)(n−j)(n−j+2))||S_j||_2||S_{n−j}||_2. **This is only an L1 nonlinear source estimate, and cannot be inserted into an L2 inverse bound.** A dimension/order-uniform L2 gradient-product bound remains missing; there is no λ=1 interacting or continuum gap conclusion.
