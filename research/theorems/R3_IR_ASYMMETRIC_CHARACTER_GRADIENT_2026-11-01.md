# Asymmetric fundamental-trace gradient bound for original-link SU2 score

For p=Re U on a single face, SU2 character polynomials U_n satisfy Γ(p,U_n)=((n+2)U_(n−1)−nU_(n+1))/2 and ||Γ(p,U_n)||²=((n+2)²+n²)/4, exactly under Haar. For every one-face class polynomial f of degree ≤d, weighted shift orthogonality yields ||Γ(p,f)||₂≤(d+1)||f||₂. More generally, on any finite original-link straight SU2 chain and for arbitrary local quaternion polynomial g of degree ≤d, pointwise Γ(p_i,p_i)=1−p_i²≤1 and Γ Cauchy–Schwarz, plus K≤6D and harmonic polynomial degree bound, imply ||Γ_m(p_i,g)||₂ ≤ sqrt(3d(d+2)/2)||g||₂, independent of chain length and support size. This removes the exponential reproducing-kernel factor from first/last partitions j=1 or k=1 of the Riccati recurrence. Balanced high-order partitions still lack polynomial L2 bilinear estimates; no ordinary convergence or interacting/continuum gap claim.
