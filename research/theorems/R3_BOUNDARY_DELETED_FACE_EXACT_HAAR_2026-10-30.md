# Exact all-orders Haar property for a deleted face with an exposed edge
Date: 2026-10-30
Status: PROVED finite-volume theorem; shows three-face boundary benchmark cannot yield non-Haar deleted vacuum.

Let a finite simply connected planar SU(2) lattice disk have strictly positive link-electric coefficients a_e. Let f have a boundary edge e0 incident to no other face. For deleted Hamiltonian H_f^-=sum_e a_e(-Delta_e)+sum_(g != f)b_g(1-p_g), the retained potential is independent of the link U_e0. On the full product-link Hilbert space H_f^-=a_e0(-Delta_e0) tensor I + I tensor H_rest, with H_rest independent of U_e0. The first summand has a unique normalized constant ground state in its SU(2) factor. The full ground state is therefore constant in U_e0. The full ground state is positive and gauge-invariant by uniqueness and gauge invariance of H; hence its physical projection is the same state. No need to assume full tensor factorization of the Gauss-invariant Hilbert space.

Given the other links, the plaquette word W_f includes U_e0 or U_e0^-1 exactly once. Left/right Haar invariance implies W_f is Haar conditional on the other links. All retained plaquette words depend only on those other links. Consequently the canonical conditional Haar projection P_f Omega_f^-=Omega_f^- and Q_f Omega_f^-=0, and for every Borel cap C in SU(2) the ground-state probability that W_f is in C equals Haar(C). The deleted face may share all its other edges with retained neighbors, and retained magnetic couplings can be arbitrary.

This applies to any connected three-rhombus finite patch with deleted f, because f cannot have its four edges all shared with only two other rhombi in an edge-to-edge rhombus disk (each pair of distinct convex rhombi shares at most one edge). Thus all third and higher-order projected perturbative coefficients vanish on that patch: the earlier second-order Fierz cancellation is a special case of exact boundary-edge decoupling. Any all-orders search for nonzero Q_f Omega must use an interior deleted face with NO exposed link. A valid Penrose test requires its complete edge-star of four retained neighbors (at least five plaquettes total). Such an embedded patch must be identified from exact incidence data, not inferred from images. These are necessary geometric conditions, not sufficiency for non-Haar response.

No uniform bulk Penrose cap estimate or continuum mass gap is asserted.
