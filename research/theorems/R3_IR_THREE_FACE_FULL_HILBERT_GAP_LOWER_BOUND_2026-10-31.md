# Rigorous three-face full-Hilbert-space gap bound

For the three linked original-link SU(2) square plaquettes with unit electric weights, at real magnetic coupling λ≥0, **Δ(H(λ)) ≥ 3−3λ+3λ²/(12+λ²)**. Proof: gauge-invariant free electric gap 3; ||Σ_i p_i||≤3, hence the first excited energy E1≥3−3λ by min-max. The explicit trial vector φ=1+(λ/3)Σ_i p_i has exact Haar norm 1+λ²/12 and Rayleigh quotient −3λ²/(12+λ²), so E0 lies below that quotient. At λ=1, **Δ(H(1))≥3/13>0** for the full, untruncated finite three-face Hamiltonian. This bound does not require extrapolating the previously computed 291-state Ritz gap ≈3.0809407568. It does not establish a chain-length uniform gap, physical continuum gap, complex analyticity or signed current. Source proof and self-contained exact verifier in Library Proof Audits.
