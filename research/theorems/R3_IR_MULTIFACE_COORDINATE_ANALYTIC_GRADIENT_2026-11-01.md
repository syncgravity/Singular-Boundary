# Multi-face SU(2) coordinate analytic carré-du-champ bound

For polynomial representatives in independent unit-quaternion coordinates on an open original-link SU2 chain, define ||f||_σ^coef=Σ_α |c_α| exp(σ|α|). Each original-link generator derivative maps a coordinate to ±(1/2) another coordinate and satisfies ||D_e f||_σ≤(deg f)/2 ||f||_σ. Each support interval of r faces touches at most 4r original links, and Γ(f,g)=Σ_{e,k}D_{e,k}f D_{e,k}g. Thus for total degrees ≤j,≤k and support sizes r_f,r_g, ||Γ(f,g)||_σ^coef ≤ 3 min(r_f,r_g) jk ||f||_σ^coef ||g||_σ^coef. For δ=σ−σ'>0, ||Γ(f,g)||_σ'^coef ≤3 min(r_f,r_g)/(e²δ²) ||f||_σ^coef||g||_σ^coef. Constants are independent of total chain length. This covers polynomial coordinate realizations of multi-face SU2 matrix coefficients, but is NOT an intrinsic Peter–Weyl norm; quotient norm, K^-1 bound in this coefficient norm, and nonlinear analytic-radius propagation remain OPEN.
