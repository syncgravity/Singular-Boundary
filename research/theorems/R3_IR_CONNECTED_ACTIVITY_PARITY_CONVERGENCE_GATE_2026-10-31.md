# Conditional connected SU(2) activity convergence gate

For an exact-support activity G_S with m active faces, SU(2) parity gives a zero of order at least 2m-q, where q=2 for overlapping dominoes and q=4 for disjoint dominoes; the diagonal positive-order activity has order at least 2m. If a common support-independent analytic radius R and boundary bound C for all connected G_S are established, then with x=|lambda|/R the rooted polymer sum is at most 4096 C x^(-q) (64x^2)^q/(1-64x^2), and converges for |lambda|<R/8. The uniform disk hypothesis is **NOT PROVED**; existing resolvent estimates yield only R_m=3/(32m), shrinking with support size.
