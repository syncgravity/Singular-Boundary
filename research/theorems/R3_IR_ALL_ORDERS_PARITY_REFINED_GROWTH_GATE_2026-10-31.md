# All-orders original-link SU(2) covariance: parity-refined connected support bound

Independent face-center parity constrains the number m of distinct active plaquettes in a nonzero total-degree-n covariance coefficient: overlapping dominoes m<=floor((n+2)/2), face-disjoint dominoes m<=floor((n+4)/2), diagonal domino variance m<=n/2 for n>=4. Odd orders vanish. At sixth order the maxima are 4,5,3 respectively. Combining this with the previously archived rooted-support counting and restricted-system resolvent/Cauchy hypotheses gives |a_n|<=72*4096*sum_{m=q}^{m_max}(128^m/2^q)*(32*m/3)^n, where q=2 for overlap/diagonal and q=4 for disjoint. This is smaller than the prior generic majorant, but still has nth-root growth proportional to n and therefore establishes no positive convergence radius. The result is conditional on the earlier analytic and linked-support ingredients; physical coupling, continuum uniformity, signed-current control and a Yang-Mills mass gap remain open.
