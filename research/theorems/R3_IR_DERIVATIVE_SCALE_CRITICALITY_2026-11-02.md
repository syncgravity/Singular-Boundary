# Derivative-scale criticality, 2026-11-02

For B(v,w)=<grad v,grad w>, the first source derivative uses gradient and Hessian inputs, but the second uses third derivatives. A naive second iteration of the archived t^(-1/2) semigroup smoothing generates a t^(-1) bound, not integrable at time zero. This is an obstruction to the argument, NOT a proof that the higher Poisson derivative bound fails. For the exact circle Laplacian and g_n=sin(nx)/n, ||g_n'||inf=1 and ||(P_t g_n)'''||inf=n²exp(-n²t); each n has integral 1 over time while sup_(lambda>0) lambda exp(-lambda*t)=1/(e*t) has divergent integral. Hence a frequency-localized resolvent argument, a Hölder/Schauder gain, or dyadic cancellation is necessary for the proposed source-locality derivative scale.
