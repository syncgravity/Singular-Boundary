# R3-IR-447 — Non-Markov exterior martingale certificate for Wilson static cap clustering

2026-10-30. Exact probability and Hilbert-space identities for the genuine finite-volume original-link Wilson ground-state measure. No Markov or spectral-gap hypothesis, but NO physical spatial decay proved.

Let mu=Omega² product-Haar be the positive normalized Wilson vacuum probability, and O=k(W_f) a bounded real gauge-invariant cap observable supported on a finite target link cage S0. For radius r choose nested exterior link sets Y_r consisting of all links at graph distance at least r from S0; Y_(r+1) subset Y_r. Write F_r for the sigma-algebra generated by Y_r, and M_r=E_mu[O|F_r]. Denote F=E_mu O. Define the exterior influence energy
I(r)=||M_r-F||²_L2(mu)=Var_mu(E_mu[O|F_r]).

THEOREM 1. For any real P measurable with respect to F_r, especially a distant retained plaquette observable whose boundary links are in Y_r,
Cov_mu(O,P)=Cov_mu(M_r,P),
and therefore
|Cov_mu(O,P)|<=sqrt(I(r) Var_mu(P)).
For bounded P, this also implies |Cov_mu(O,P)|<=||P-E P||_2 sqrt(I(r)).
This is a distance-sensitive *certificate*: a Wilson-specific estimate I(r)<=C exp(-2mr) would prove exponential static cap clustering with exponent m, but that estimate is NOT established.

THEOREM 2. Since conditional expectations are orthogonal projections and F_(r+1) is a sub-sigma-algebra of F_r, M_r is a reverse martingale and satisfies the exact Pythagorean identity
I(r)-I(r+1)=||M_r-M_(r+1)||²_L2(mu)>=0.
For integers R>r,
I(r)=I(R)+sum_(j=r)^(R-1)||M_j-M_(j+1)||².
In finite volume once Y_R is empty, M_R=F and I(R)=0, so I(r) is EXACTLY the sum of remaining squared shell-influence increments. Positivity of individual increments does not establish geometric suppression or a uniform r-bound.

THEOREM 3. For ANY bounded cap O, 0<=I(r)<=Var_mu(O)<=||O||_infty². R3-IR-446 controls the total oscillation of the local conditional cap expectation against arbitrary exterior configurations, but provides neither an r-dependent estimate on I(r) nor a decay ratio I(r+1)/I(r). In particular a uniform one-block Doeblin minorant cannot be multiplied across shells without additional conditional independence.

Boundary and gauge detail: sigma-algebras are taken on original-link configurations before quotienting by gauge; conditional expectations of a gauge-invariant O under the gauge-invariant positive mu exist without gauge fixing. The inequalities are probability identities and their gauge-invariant observable correlations are physical. Exterior sigma-algebras need not individually be gauge invariant, which only makes I(r) potentially larger; it does not invalidate the covariance bound.

This establishes a precise *static* non-Markov multiscale criterion, not a spatial mixing bound. It does not control the long-time reduced resolvent in R3-IR-441, prove half-Haar target cap survival, or establish the 3+1D continuum Yang-Mills mass gap.
