# Dressed geometric H1-to-H3 resolvent via weighted-divergence cancellation — 2026-11-02

For reversible L=−Delta−2grad S·grad on the finite SU(2) strip, u=R Q f and H=Hess u, the exact divergence identity is **div_nu H=−grad(Qf)+(Ric−2Hess S)grad u**. Proof: div_nu Hess u=grad Delta u+Ric grad u+2H(grad S); Delta u=−Qf−2grad S·grad u, and grad(grad S·grad u)=Hess S grad u+H(grad S). The drift terms cancel exactly. Thus **<H,Hess(Qf)>_nu=||grad f||²+<grad f,(2Hess S−Ric)grad u>_nu**. This removes the source-Hessian requirement from the preceding dressed geometric third-derivative estimate. It is unweighted dressed L2, not link-local or pointwise, and does not prove nonlinear contraction at lambda=1 or a continuum Yang-Mills mass gap.
