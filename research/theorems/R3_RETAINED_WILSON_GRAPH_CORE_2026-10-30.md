# Fixed-regulator retained Wilson cores: electric form and operator source limits
Date: 2026-10-30
Status: PROVED qualitative finite-regulator approximation; quantitative Penrose-uniform cost and continuum gap OPEN.

Setup: on a finite simply connected planar SU(2) link disk, after a maximal-tree gauge fix, independent based-face holonomies (u,Y) carry product Haar with residual simultaneous conjugation. The physical deleted-face Haar projection P averages u, Q=I-P. H_-=T+V_- with T=sum_e a_e(-Delta_e), a_e>0 fixed; Omega is the smooth positive gauge-invariant ground state and p=P Omega is smooth, retained-coordinate dependent and conjugation invariant. Finite Wilson-polynomial / spin-network retained functions mean finite matrix-coefficient sums of Y, projected onto simultaneous-conjugation invariants; this is broader than products of individual face traces.

THEOREM 1 (finite regulator core). There is a sequence p_N of finite retained gauge-invariant Peter-Weyl polynomials such that ||p_N-p||_L2² + E_T[p_N-p] ->0, E_T[z]=sum_e a_e||grad_e z||². Justification: Peter-Weyl Fourier partial sums on compact retained product group approximate a smooth p in all fixed Sobolev orders. Conjugation averaging is bounded and preserves smoothness; p is invariant. The smooth finite-dimensional coordinate change between link and based-face systems transports fixed-order convergence, with constants depending on the regulator/tree. This is fixed-regulator qualitative, not an h-uniform rate or support bound.

THEOREM 2 (source form convergence). On physical Q-sector test v in electric form domain, define the source distribution s_N=-QTP p_N, s=-QTP p (where P p_N=p_N). Then <v,s_N-s>=-sum_e a_e <grad_e v,grad_e(p_N-p)> so |<v,s_N-s>|<=E_T[v]^(1/2) E_T[p_N-p]^(1/2). This proves convergence in the dual of the electric form domain, with possible harmless L2 term added to define a Hilbert dual norm. Pure H1 convergence DOES NOT imply ||s_N-s||_L2->0.

THEOREM 3 (stronger source convergence). Because the finite-regulator physical T is a second-order elliptic differential operator with smooth coefficients in the retained smooth coordinate chart, choosing retained polynomial approximants p_N converging to p in the fixed-regulator H² Sobolev norm gives ||T(p_N-p)||_L2->0, and consequently ||s_N-s||_L2<=||T(p_N-p)||_L2->0. For a full smooth p, convergence can be taken in all fixed Sobolev orders. This does NOT imply the stronger source convergence is uniform in h: the coordinate map, electric weights, graph size, and required Wilson word complexity can deteriorate.

For the fixed finite lattice, A=Q(H_--E)Q on Ran Q has lambda_Q>0 and q_N=A^-1 s_N converges in L2 to q=A^-1 s. Quantitatively ||q_N-q||<=lambda_Q^-1 ||s_N-s||, and for any cap multiplication M_delta, ||M_delta(q_N-q)||<=lambda_Q^-1||s_N-s||. This estimate becomes ineffective if lambda_Q deteriorates with refinement. It is not proof of uniform cap survival.

FINITE SOURCE EXPANSANSION: Each retained gauge-invariant polynomial p_N can be written as a finite smooth function of finitely many real/imaginary matrix-coefficient invariant coordinates B_i(Y), locally or using a finite polynomial embedding. The earlier chain-rule formula -Q T Phi(B)=-sum_i partial_i Phi Q(TB_i)+sum_(i,j)partial_ij Phi Q Gamma_T(B_i,B_j) applies when its B_i and Phi are smooth and retained. Rooted Wilson link-word supports and coefficients can grow with approximation degree; nearest-neighbor trace cancellation is NOT a universal property.

CONCLUSION: The algebra-to-vacuum extension is mathematically rigorous at each fixed finite regulator in H1 and (with H2 approximants) source L2, but the missing quantitative theorem is a jointly uniform approximation rate and electric source-response estimate at cap scale delta~h. No Penrose polynomial cap lower bound, Wilson continuum construction, or mass gap proved.
