It flips W_f to -W_f while preserving each retained Wilson plaquette holonomy and the Hamiltonian itself.

E_vac[h(W_f)]=E_vac[h(-W_f)]. (CS-1)

The conclusion also holds for gauge-projected heat traces, since S_z commutes with the physical projector and H.
