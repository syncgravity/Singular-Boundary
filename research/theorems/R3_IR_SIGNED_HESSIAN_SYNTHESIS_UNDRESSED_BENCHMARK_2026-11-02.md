# Signed Hessian synthesis: undressed theorem and dressed Gram identity

On the finite open SU(2) strip, neighboring-bond residual w_j depends on S_j=E_j union E_(j+1), seven original links. At zero dressing, the product Laplacian and its centered Poisson resolvent preserve source coordinate support. For each original link e, at most three (vertical) or two (horizontal) bond resolvents have a nonzero Hessian row. Pointwise therefore |sum_j Hess_(e,*) R_0Q w_j| <= sqrt(3)*(sum_j|Hess_(e,*)R_0Qw_j|²)^1/2, and the same inequality holds in L4; the factor is independent of strip length. At nonzero dressing the weighted resolvent is nonlocal and the argument does not apply. The dressed exact polarized Bochner formula is <Hess h_i,Hess h_j>_2=<Qw_i,Qw_j>_2 - int (Ric-2Hess S_g)(grad h_i,grad h_j)dnu. It gives signed global L2 cross correlations but not signed local L4 bounds. The needed dressed synthesis or perturbative tail theorem remains unproved.
