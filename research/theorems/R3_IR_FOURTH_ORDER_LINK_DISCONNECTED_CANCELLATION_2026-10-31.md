# R3-IR — Fourth-order link-disconnected covariance cancellation

For the finite planar original-link SU(2) Wilson family with independent plaquette couplings, two domino-loop traces with disjoint enclosed face regions and no shared original boundary links have **exactly zero fourth derivative** of connected covariance at zero common magnetic coupling. Under the planar center-sign symmetry, at degree four the only allowable joint coupling monomial consists of the four distinct plaquette couplings in the two dominoes. Switching off all other couplings, the original-link Hamiltonian decomposes into two independent magnetic subsystems and spectators, so its unique positive ground state factorizes and the connected covariance vanishes identically. Therefore the coefficient of the only admissible fourth-order monomial is zero. This proof requires disjoint link support; it does not cover face-disjoint but edge-adjacent dominoes.

Reproducible graph audit over square disks N=3,5,7,9,11,15,21: for N=21, 343,866 out of 352,380 distinct domino pairs have disjoint link supports and are certified fourth-order zero; 6,116 disjoint but edge-adjacent pairs and 2,398 overlapping pairs are not settled by this theorem. This is a fourth-order connected-cluster **support theorem**, not a fourth-order coefficient evaluation of the remaining pairs, a uniform convergence proof, or a physical-coupling Yang–Mills mass-gap result. Signed center current, covariance decay and continuum gap remain OPEN.
