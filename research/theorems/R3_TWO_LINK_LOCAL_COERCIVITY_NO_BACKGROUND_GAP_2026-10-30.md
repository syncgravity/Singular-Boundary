# Two-link local coercivity for deleted-face Haar response

Date: 2026-10-30. Scope: open SU(2) disk with product-link Haar, positive electric coefficients, and a deleted target face f. This is not a refinement-uniform theorem or continuum mass-gap proof.

Let g be a retained face adjacent to f. Assume (i) a link e is shared by f and g, and is contained in no other face; (ii) a second link d of g lies on the free external boundary and belongs to no other retained face; (iii) after switching off the sole retained magnetic coupling b_g, the magnetic potential is independent of both e and d; (iv) the canonical deleted-face conditional Haar fiber can be realized by varying only these two links, keeping all other retained based-face holonomies fixed. This is verified for the extracted eleven-face M5 disk, with pairs (e,d)=(0,86),(1,88),(4,372),(13,26).

On the full product-link L2 before Gauss projection, define P=P_e P_d as the commuting Haar averages over both link variables, and R=I-P. Let H0=T+V_other denote the Hamiltonian with b_g=0 and E0 its ground energy. H0 commutes with P, because it contains only independent Casimir electric terms in links e and d; on R, at least one free link carries nonzero SU2 representation and has Casimir >=3/4. Put gamma_loc=(3/4)*min(a_e,a_d). Since the remaining operator has ground energy E0 (tensor product Haar constants on e,d), R(H0-E0)R >= gamma_loc R. The global gap of the other links is irrelevant.

Restore H=H0-b_g p_g for b_g>=0, with ||p_g||<=1 and P p_g P=0 since p_g has one fundamental occurrence on d. Its normalized ground state Omega has E<=E0 by the variational test on the ground state of H0 (the expectation of p_g vanishes on link d). Project the eigenvector equation onto R: [R(H0-E)R-b_g R p_g R]R Omega=b_g R p_g P Omega. Thus (gamma_loc-b_g)||R Omega||<=b_g||P Omega||<=b_g. Since P functions are invariant along the deleted Haar fiber, Q_f P=0, so ||Q_f Omega||<=||R Omega||<=b_g/(gamma_loc-b_g) for b_g<gamma_loc. This proof uses NO global spectral gap of H0.

The usual double-center cap bound m_f(delta)>=[sqrt(c_delta)*sqrt(1-eps^2)-eps]_+^2 follows with eps=min(1,b_g/(gamma_loc-b_g)) and c_delta=2/pi[arccos(1-delta)-(1-delta)*sqrt(2delta-delta^2)]. For unit a_e=a_d=1 and b_g=3/32000, eps=1/7999. At delta=0.1, m_f(0.1)>0.03733774. This is weaker than the special four-coupling perturbative estimate but stronger than the previously committed switched-off global-gap comparison.

Refinement issue: at delta proportional to h, this method needs b_g/a_min small enough that eps is O(h^(3/4)), whereas physically scaled b_g/a_min may not tend to zero. A local coercivity bound is NOT a refinement-uniform Yang-Mills cap theorem without additional scaling control. Explicit geometry assumptions cannot be asserted for an arbitrary periodic torus or arbitrary refined patch without verifying boundary structure.
