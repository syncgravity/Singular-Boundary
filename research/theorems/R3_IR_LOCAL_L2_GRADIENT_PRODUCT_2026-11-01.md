# Local L2 gradient-product estimate for original-link SU(2) chains

For quaternion polynomials f,g of total degrees ≤j,≤k supported on r_f,r_g faces, respectively, on any finite open SU(2) original-link chain, let N(j,r)=C(j+4r,j). The original-link carré du champ satisfies ||Γ(f,g)||_2 ≤ (3/2)sqrt(j(j+2)k(k+2))min(sqrt(N(j,r_f)),sqrt(N(k,r_g)))||f||_2||g||_2. If r_f≤j,r_g≤k, this is ≤(3/2)sqrt(j(j+2)k(k+2))2^{(5/2)min(j,k)}||f||_2||g||_2. Proof: the finite-degree polynomial subspace on SU(2)^r is translation-stable with dimension ≤C(j+4r,j); its reproducing kernel has constant diagonal, so ||v||∞≤sqrt(N)||v||2, also for differentiated polynomials. The physical shared-link quadratic-form bound Γ(h,h)≤6|∇h|² and polynomial Casimir/Bernstein inequality ⟨h,Dh⟩≤j(j+2)||h||²/4 give the result. This is a **local** L2 upgrade of the previously derived L1 source estimate, uniform in ambient chain length but exponentially growing in order; it does not close the all-orders connected recurrence or establish λ=1 gap. Mathematical proof, exact combinatorial verifier, moment check and log accompany the Proof Audits ZIP.
