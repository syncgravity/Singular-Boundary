# Connected-support Sobolev threshold for quadratic gradient sources — 2026-11-02

For a fixed SU(2)^k block, d=3k, the quadratic source product ∇<∇u,∇v> maps H^s×H^s into L² whenever integer s≥2 and **s>3/2+d/4** by Sobolev multiplication H^{s−2}H^{s−1}→L². Below that threshold a concentration bump u_e=eps^{s−d/2}phi(x/eps) has bounded H^s and ||∇|∇u_e|²||₂~eps^{2s−3−d/2}→∞. On three links d=9, H⁴ suffices while H³ fails. A connected t-face open strip interval has 3t+1 links and d(t)=9t+3, requiring s>9(t+1)/4 for this fixed-dimensional argument. Thus no fixed finite isotropic H^s closes the unrestricted quadratic source on all growing intervals. Endpoint equality is not resolved. This does not exclude support-size-dependent analytic/Peter-Weyl or tensorized polymer norms, and proves neither a nonlinear λ=1 gap nor continuum Yang-Mills mass gap.
