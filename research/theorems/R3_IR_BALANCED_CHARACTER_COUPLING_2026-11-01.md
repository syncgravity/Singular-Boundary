# Balanced SU(2) character-gradient coupling and analytic radius loss

For a single face p=Re U, Haar-orthonormal SU(2) characters U_n have KU_n=n(n+2)U_n and product U_mU_n=Σ_{l=|m−n|,step2}^{m+n} U_l. By the K product rule, Γ(U_m,U_n)=1/2 Σ_l[m(m+2)+n(n+2)−l(l+2)]U_l. Exact orthogonality gives ||Γ(U_n,U_n)||²=n(n+1)(n+2)(7n²+14n+4)/15, asymptotic to (7/15)n^5. Thus balanced one-face character interactions grow polynomially (L2 norm n^(5/2)), not exponentially. For weighted analytic character norm ||f||_σ=Σ|f_n|e^(σn), and δ=σ−σ'>0, the exact finite Clebsch–Gordan sum yields ||Γ(f,g)||_(σ') ≤ [32+108/(e³δ³)]||f||_σ||g||_σ. This loses analytic radius; there is no claim of a same-radius bounded bilinear map or a global multi-face spin-network recurrence.
