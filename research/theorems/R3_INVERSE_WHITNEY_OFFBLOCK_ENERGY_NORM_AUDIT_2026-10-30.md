# R3 off-block electric coupling: exact energy bound, not an L2 operator bound (2026-10-30)

For the assembled 11-face 30-link SPD Whitney mass matrix M let K=M^-1. Split the link indices into a selected local pair L and background B. Write K in blocks A=K_LL, C=K_LB, B0=K_BB. By SPD, the singular-value coupling alpha=||A^-1/2 C B0^-1/2||_op is STRICTLY less than one. For arbitrary transported derivative vectors u_L,u_B, define energies E_L=u_L* A u_L and E_B=u_B* B0 u_B. The precise Cauchy-Schwarz cross form bound is |u_L* C u_B| <= alpha sqrt(E_L E_B). Consequently u* K u >= (1-alpha)(E_L+E_B). This is a quadratic FORM estimate; it does not prove that R H0 P is bounded on L2, nor that the free-link Haar projector P reduces H0.
