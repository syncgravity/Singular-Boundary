# Pointwise dressed Hessian semigroup extension and nonlinear bootstrap audit

The archived dressed W2,4 Duhamel proof extends to L-infinity, because the reverse Poincare inequality for scalar Markov semigroups gives a pointwise derivative bound on each forced component, the Markov semigroup is L-infinity contractive, the dressing forcing is pointwise controlled by M times the weighted full gradient, and the compatible weighted gradient has L-infinity decay. Under the previous geometric and Bochner certificates and q_g<333723/1e9, one obtains c=0.4983981296, M=0.0019355934, K≈1.86485605087, delta=c/4, and **||Hess Rg||_(a,infinity)≤9.364013781||grad g||_(a,infinity)** for every admissible original-link weight and all finite strip sizes. Exact source-shell locality gives pointwise zero-coupling per-link estimates: |grad_e u1|<0.579319, |Hess_(e,*)u1|<2.703686, |grad_v|grad u1|²|<192.423, |grad_h|grad u1|²|<183.952 and |Hess_(e,*)u2|<13413.100. This strengthens earlier L4-only reports and makes the pointwise reference regularity available conditionally.

**Not a nonlinear fixed-point closure:** a local per-row bound does not alone furnish a uniform global Hessian operator norm or a bilinear Poisson map constant; naive contraction against the global gradient costs sqrt(number of links). The first-response row upper bound already exceeds the curvature smallness threshold ~0.24933. Consequently neither the uniform lambda=1 interacting gap nor a continuum Yang-Mills mass gap is proved.
