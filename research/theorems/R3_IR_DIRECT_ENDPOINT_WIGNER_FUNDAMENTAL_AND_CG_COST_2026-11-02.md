# Direct endpoint Wigner bound in all-fundamental degree sector — 2026-11-02

On a straight open SU(2) face chain with t>=2 and PW degrees n_i=1 on every face, the shared-link coupling C has Wigner matrix [[1/4,0,0,0],[0,-1/4,1/2,0],[0,1/2,-1/4,0],[0,0,0,1/4]], so ||C||_(1->1)=3/4. For K_t=3t I-2 sum_(i=1)^(t-1) C_i, Neumann summation in the ORIGINAL uncoupled Wigner coefficient basis yields ||K_t^(-1)||_1<=2/[3(t+1)] and therefore **||K_t^(-1)C_b||_(1->1)<=1/[2(t+1)]**. Exact rational block inversion gives norms 1/10, 3/40, 3/50 for t=2,3,4.

Independently, diagonalizing all t-1 commuting edge couplings yields ||K_t^(-1) C_b||_(spin coefficient l1)-> <=1/4 uniformly for arbitrary PW degree profiles (from the archived spin-projector eigenvalue bound). Yet the uncoupled-to-coupled CG change-of-basis l1 norm is exactly 2^((t-1)/2) in both directions on the all-degree-one block, and C_b is invertible on this block. Thus a naive spin-to-Wigner norm conversion has exponential support-length loss even on the operator's full image. This is a conversion-method obstruction, NOT a lower bound on the direct Wigner endpoint operator. A degree-uniform direct Wigner estimate for high uneven profiles, connected-polymer convergence, interacting lambda=1 gap and continuum Yang–Mills mass gap remain open.
