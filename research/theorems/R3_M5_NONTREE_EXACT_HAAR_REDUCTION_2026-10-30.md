# R3-IR-527 — Exact Haar reduction for non-tree links of the rooted inverse-Whitney electric operator

Date: 2026-10-30. Scope: **the previously specified rooted-tree parallel-transport prescription** for the finite SU(2) inverse-Whitney quadratic-form operator, on the actual extracted M5 11-face open disk. No claim of transport-path-choice independence or a continuum gap is made.

Let G=(V,E) be a connected finite oriented graph, T a rooted primal spanning tree, and V_j(U) the Wilson transporter along the unique path in T from root to source(j). Let D_j=Ad(V_j)X_j, where X_j are source-covariant Haar-skew-adjoint invariant link derivatives. For any edge e outside T, every coefficient Ad(V_j) is independent of U_e, including j=e. Let P_e be integration in the U_e coordinate with all other links fixed. For j≠e, both X_j and Ad(V_j) commute with P_e. For j=e, P_e X_e=0=X_e P_e, and Ad(V_e) is independent of U_e, so P_e D_e=D_e P_e=0. Thus **[P_e,D_j]=0** on smooth functions for all j, and the commutation extends to D_j* (Haar adjoints) and the closed form t=(g2²/2) Σ_ij K_ij <D_i psi,D_j psi>, where K is any fixed real positive definite matrix. Consequently P_e commutes with the self-adjoint Friedrichs operator H_E=D* K D. It is a reducing projection on the entire Hilbert space and on the gauge-invariant subspace (gauge transformations preserve coordinate Haar averaging).

If the remaining magnetic multiplication V_rest is independent of U_e, P_e also commutes with H0=H_E+V_rest. The unique strictly positive ground state Omega0 satisfies P_e Omega0=Omega0: P_e Omega0 is a nonzero nonnegative ground eigenfunction by positivity, and simplicity forces equality (normalization). Therefore the joint ground-state density |Omega0|² is independent of U_e and its **conditional U_e law is exactly Haar**, even with arbitrarily strong other magnetic couplings. For any plaquette g containing e exactly once, while its coupling b_g is set to zero and e occurs in no other magnetic plaquette, the conditional g-holonomy law is also Haar (by fixed left/right SU(2) translations), giving exactly the Haar double-center cap mass c_delta for the switched-off ground state. This is more informative than merely center-flip parity R3-IR-513 and does not require a global electric-vacuum comparison.

The archived disk has 20 vertices, 30 links, 19 rooted-tree links and **11 non-tree links** for deterministic BFS root vertex 0. The free outer non-tree links and retained face assignments are: 11→8,25→7,26→10,34→11,86→1,88→2,89→3,295→150,300→154,372→4 (face values are full M5 approximant face indices). Script research/scripts/validate_m5_nontree_haar_reduction.py verifies incidence and that no root-to-source path includes any non-tree link. In particular e=86 (face 1) and e=88 (face 2) give exact switched-off Haar fibers even under arbitrarily strong environment magnetic terms. The list also has another free candidate e=372 (face 4) and e=26 (face 10) from the earlier four-neighbor analysis; all four adjacent retained faces have a free non-tree edge under this root tree.

IMPORTANT: This establishes [P_e,H_E]=0 only for non-tree e, and only for this tree-path prescription. It does NOT establish [P_e,H_E]=0 for selected tree edges 0,1,4, or prove the commutator is nonzero there. It does NOT yield a positive local excitation sector gap uniform in strong magnetic b_g, nor directly prove the **deleted face 0** Haar component (its shared link is a different edge). In particular the b_g=0 switched-off Haar law for g does not itself imply a cap theorem for the deleted-face holonomy after coupling g back on. No refinement-uniform center-cap bound or continuum Yang–Mills mass gap follows.
