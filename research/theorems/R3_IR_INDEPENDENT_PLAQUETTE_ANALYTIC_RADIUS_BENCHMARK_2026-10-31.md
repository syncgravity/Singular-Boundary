# Independent-plaquette analyticity radius benchmark

For m original-link-disjoint SU(2) plaquettes, the single-character electric spectrum is 4j(j+1), the elementary normalized trace is p=chi_(1/2)/2, and ||p||<=1. The free one-face gap is 3, giving an exact Riesz-local analytic disk |z|<3/2. The m-copy real ground state and local expectations factorize; all local observables inherit this same disk. By contrast, a global operator bound ||sum p_i||<=m certifies only |z|<3/(2m). Thus 1/m shrinkage is artificial for factorized local responses. Exact low-order coefficients: [z^2]E=-1/12, [z]<p>=1/6. Independent finite 18-character truncation checks real gap for lambda=0,0.25,0.5,1,1.5. **Limitation:** Does NOT establish a uniform isolated complex eigenprojection of the entire m-copy operator, nor any uniform connected activity bound for link-sharing plaquettes, physical-coupling clustering, signed-current estimate, or 3D Yang–Mills mass gap.
