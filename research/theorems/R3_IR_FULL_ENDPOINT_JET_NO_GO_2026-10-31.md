# R3-IR — Full endpoint-jet no-go for unweighted radial current

2026-10-31. **Status:** exact constructive functional-analytic obstruction; not a counterexample in the physical deleted-face Wilson ground state. Strengthens the earlier 2026-10-30 cap-capacity result by fixing the *full endpoint jets, including values and center parity*, while the current diverges.

For every M>0, there exists a smooth strictly positive class amplitude ψ on SU(2), satisfying ψ(u)=ψ(π-u), Haar normalization ∫_0^π (2/π)sin²u ψ(u)²du=1, and ψ(u)=1 in neighborhoods of u=0 and u=π, but with one-sided center current T=∫_0^(π/2)[∂u logψ]_+du≥M. In particular every derivative of ψ at both endpoints, including the 0th derivative, agrees with the constant Haar amplitude. No bound on the current follows from the entire center jets alone.

**Construction.** Choose 0<a<π/16, a smooth increasing flat step η with η=0 for t≤0 and η=1 for t≥1. Let d_a(u)=-M[η((u-a)/a)-η((u-3a)/a)], a smooth negative well supported on [a,4a], reaching -M on [2a,3a]. Put v(u)=d_a(u)+d_a(π-u). Choose a nonnegative, nonzero smooth symmetric bump b(u), supported inside [π/4,3π/4] and zero near both endpoints. Since ∫w exp(2v)<1, while ∫w exp(2(v+cb))→∞ as c→∞, continuity yields a unique c>0 with ∫w exp(2(v+cb))=1. Set ψ=exp(v+cb). It is normalized, positive, class-smooth, parity symmetric, and identically 1 near each endpoint. On [a,4a], the logarithm descends from 0 to -M and returns to 0, yielding positive variation ≥M. The central positive bump can increase but not decrease this variation.

This demonstrates that no argument based solely on center endpoint jets (including those of any finite order, or the entire germ) suffices for the one-sided score budget. It does **not** disprove any Wilson-specific differential inequality controlling the score over a nonzero collar; the constructed amplitude need not be a ground state of a Wilson plaquette Hamiltonian. The actual obligation is a regulator-uniform Wilson ground-state *collar* control and anti-depletion, not a further center Taylor coefficient.
