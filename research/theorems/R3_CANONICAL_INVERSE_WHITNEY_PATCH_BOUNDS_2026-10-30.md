# Canonical inverse Whitney metric and two-sided finite-patch bounds — 2026-10-30

A discretized quadratic velocity Lagrangian L_kin=(1/(2g²)) dot(q)^T M dot(q) with symmetric SPD Whitney mass matrix M yields canonical p=g^-2 M dot(q), hence H_kin=(g²/2) p^T M^-1 p, not (g²/2) p^T M p. For nonabelian lattice gauge theory, this is conditional on a consistent choice of gauge-covariant velocity variables, canonical cotangent trivialization and measure: noncommuting momentum operators and configuration-dependent transports require an ordered, positive quadratic-form quantization. It does not by itself identify the physical Penrose Hamiltonian.

If each oriented four-edge Whitney Schur matrix S_f obeys c_tile I <= S_f <= C_tile I for constants uniform over nondegenerate allowed tile shapes, and a physical link occurs in at least one and at most two open-disk faces, the assembled M_P obeys c_tile I <= M_P <= 2 C_tile I. Proof: x^T M_P x=sum_f x_f^T S_f x_f, while sum_f ||x_f||²=sum_e n_e |x_e|² with 1<=n_e<=2. Taking inverses reverses operator order: (2 C_tile)^-1 I <= M_P^-1 <= c_tile^-1 I. This holds for arbitrarily large assemblies with the same shape family. Uniform geometric rescaling in two spatial dimensions leaves Whitney 1-form mass matrices scale invariant. This geometric bound does NOT address volume growth of magnetic interactions or the Yang-Mills mass gap.

Numerical regression on the PREVIOUSLY assembled 30-link 11-face open disk (double precision, NOT interval-certified): spectrum M approximately [0.14317414869400802,1.1046593441618966]; spectrum M^-1 approximately [0.90525645,6.98450111]. Above 1e-10 threshold M has 132 off-diagonal ordered nonzeros, whereas M^-1 has 870 of possible 870. Thus inverse metric is dense; replacing it by independently inverted face contributions or diagonal edge factors changes the discretization. In particular inversion does NOT commute with facewise assembly or Schur elimination.

A genuine SU2 operator can be made positive at finite regulator by taking a derivative column D with carefully chosen gauge-covariant transported components and the form <D psi,M_P^-1 D psi>. However if the velocity-based canonical momentum matrix contains configuration-dependent adjoint transports, one must derive which covariance matrix is inverted and include correct Jacobian and operator ordering. The previously established form using M_P directly is a different candidate, not automatically the canonical one. Gauge invariance and closability remain to be verified for a specified full construction. No interacting shrinking-center cap or continuum mass gap is proved.
