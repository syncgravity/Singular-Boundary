# Collective dual-path electric commutator and exact f-sum identity
Date: 2026-10-30
Status: exact smooth finite-dimensional operator/form identities; small connected Penrose response OPEN.

Assume the nonabelian Haar-preserving SU(2) dual-path action T_g with generators D_{gamma,a} (a=1,2,3), as in OT-1/OT-3, on the full product-link manifold. H_-=T+V_- with T=sum_e a_e(-Delta_e), physical -Delta chi_j=j(j+1)chi_j, Omega>0 normalized, H_-Omega=E Omega. Work on smooth functions; extension to relevant graph and quadratic form domains must be justified separately.

1. Potential invariance entails D_a V_-=0 and [H_-,D_a]=[T,D_a]. Therefore (H_--E)D_a Omega=[T,D_a]Omega. This does not imply the RHS is small.
2. The Haar-preserving action makes D_a skew-adjoint. Put K=H_--E. Then
<Omega,[D_a,[H_-,D_a]]Omega>=-2<D_a Omega,K D_a Omega>.
Proof: [D,[H,D]]=2DHD-D²H-HD². With H Omega=E Omega and D*= -D, the expectation equals -2 <D Omega,(H-E)D Omega>. Hence the LHS is nonpositive.
3. Ground-state transform: <D_a Omega,K D_a Omega>=integral Omega² Gamma_a(D_a log Omega,D_a log Omega) dm. As D_a Omega=Omega D_a log Omega, this is exact in the smooth positive setting. Therefore the double commutator gives a nonnegative energy-weighted score response; it is NOT a bound on J_gamma=sum ||D_a Omega||² absent an independently proved spectral gap/coercivity on these derivative vectors.
4. Principal symbol of [T,D_a] is generally second order: for the positive Laplace operator T associated with product metric and a vector field D, the principal order-two term is generated by Lie derivative of the inverse metric along D (equivalently failure of D to be Killing). It vanishes for the central transformation but not for a generic noncentral dual-path generator, as exhibited in OT-3. Magnetic-preserving transport is not an electric isometry. There is no justified cancellation of this order-two source along a growing dual path.
5. Conditional spectral response: if each D_a Omega is orthogonal to ker K and supported spectrally in [lambda, infinity), then sum ||D_a Omega||² <=lambda^-1 sum<D_a Omega,K D_a Omega>. For a finite compact Hamiltonian with simple ground state, <Omega,D_a Omega>=0 by skew-adjointness, so orthogonality holds. But regulator-uniform lambda is not established and treating it as given would risk circular use of a mass-gap assumption. A localized spectral decomposition may instead split low and high energies, but controlling low-energy overlap remains open.
6. No polynomial deleted cap probability, uniform orbit Fisher smallness, bulk deletion comparison or continuum mass gap follows from these identities alone.

Proof provenance: SU(2) dual-path action per OT-1 and OT-3, integration by parts, ground-state equation, and standard ground-state transform. This is an analytic finite-volume note; no historical Stage 26 data changed.
