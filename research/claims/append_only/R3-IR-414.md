# R3-IR-414 — Complete physical spin-one curvature bound

Proved at last-neighbor zero coupling: the complete two-resolvent-channel SU(2) Wilson vacuum response satisfies |c1''(0)|<=4(b_g/a_e)^2. Hence any central cap k with spin-one coefficient a1 satisfies |F_k''(0)|<=4|a1|b_g²/(3a_e²). This uses private-link kinematical gaps, not the interacting physical gap. No favorable sign or full-coupling bound is obtained.
