# R3-IR-401 — Physical heat-trace cap baseline error

Proved fixed finite volume: for positive O bounded by nu, the Gauss-projected thermal cap expectation differs from its vacuum value by at most nu D_beta/(1+D_beta), where D_beta is the excited physical heat trace. D_beta <= D_beta0 exp(-(beta-beta0)delta).

Source: research/theorems/R3_IR_PHYSICAL_HEAT_TRACE_CAP_BASELINE_ERROR_2026-10-30.md.

At a private-link ground-state Haar baseline, relative error requires D_beta small compared with alpha_a squared divided by nu_a. No uniform refinement bound or full-coupling sign result is claimed.
