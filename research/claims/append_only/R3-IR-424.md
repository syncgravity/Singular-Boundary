# R3-IR-424 — Higher-spin four-neighbor Wilson insertion threshold

Proved finite-volume on original-link SU(2) Wilson disk: for target integer spin j, each neighbor coupling derivative of order n<2j vanishes at that neighbor's decoupled boundary, even with the other three couplings active. Exact full-coupling reconstruction uses mixed derivative order 2j in each of four couplings (total order 8j) against nonnegative Taylor-remainder weights. This is a high-spin selection rule, not a bound on the mixed derivative, cap survival, or the continuum mass gap.
