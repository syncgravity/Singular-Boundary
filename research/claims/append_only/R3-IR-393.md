# R3-IR-393 — Signed source alignment obstruction

Status: exact finite-volume signed spectral representation and reversible two-state countermodel; Wilson-specific cap-response sign is OPEN.

Source: [signed alignment note](../../theorems/R3_IR_SIGNED_SOURCE_SPECTRAL_ALIGNMENT_OBSTRUCTION_2026-10-30.md).

The cross-source resolvent overlap is the inverse-energy integral of a signed, not positive, spectral measure. Positive reversible dynamics can have either sign: an explicit two-state example has overlap -1/8 for opposite monotone source vectors and +1/8 for aligned vectors. This is not a physical SU(2) Wilson counterexample. The Wilson completion transport lower bound and continuum mass gap remain OPEN.
