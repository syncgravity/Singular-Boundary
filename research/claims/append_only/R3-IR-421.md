# R3-IR-421 — Center-symmetric spin-one cap certificate

For the physical deleted-target Wilson vacuum on a planar disk with free boundary, exact center symmetry and `3-chi_1(U)=4sin²(theta)` give `P(B_a(I)) >= (1/2)[1-(3-c_1)/(4sin²a)]_+`. A sufficient half-Haar cap condition is `3-c_1 <= 4sin²a(1-Haar(B_a))`. This requires a near-maximal spin-one expectation at angular scale a~sqrt(h), not mere positivity. No such completed-cage Wilson deficit estimate is proved.
