# R3-IR-429 — Weak-ratio Wilson cap Haar stability

From the exact full-environment private-link semigroup bound and SU(2) character heat-kernel expansion, the target cap ratio is at least `exp[-(16/3)R log(A/R)-8R]` for sufficiently small `R=b_g/a_x>0`, with a universal finite `A`. This tends to one as R tends to zero and gives an explicit half-Haar sufficient inequality. It does not apply uniformly to physical refinements with large R.
