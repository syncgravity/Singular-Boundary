# R3-IR-425 — Full-coupling local electric spin tail

For a target-neighbor shared original link x, with deleted target magnetic face and only the neighboring face g magnetically depending on x, the true Wilson ground state obeys `a_x < -Delta_x > <= b_g` at any neighboring coupling, including full strength. Consequently the Peter-Weyl link-spin tail obeys `||P_{ell>=s} Omega||^2 <= min(1,b_g/[a_x s(s+1)])`. The proof is variational and independent of environmental volume and interacting spectral gap. This does not control the completed-cage small-cap probability at physical scaling.
