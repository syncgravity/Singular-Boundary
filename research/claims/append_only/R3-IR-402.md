# R3-IR-402 — Explicit planar center-flux construction

Finite-disk theorem: mod-two face-edge incidence of a cellulation of a topological disk with independent boundary links has full row rank. Hence an SU(2) link-center transformation can reverse precisely one selected face plaquette while preserving target and all other face holonomies. The physical electric operator and Gauss projector are invariant. This validates the independent center-flux hypothesis of FN-2 for ordinary disk boundary conditions; it does not imply signed cap survival.
