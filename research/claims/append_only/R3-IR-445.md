R3-IR-445. A three-link SU(2) rotor with only nearest-neighbor interactions has a positive equal-time ground-state density that is not a nearest-neighbor Markov field. Exact second-order perturbation shows a nonzero x-z term in the log density after Haar averaging over the intermediate link. This is a diagnostic rotor example, not an original-link gauge Wilson vacuum. Spatial locality of the Hamiltonian alone cannot justify finite-range Dobrushin shell conditionals.
