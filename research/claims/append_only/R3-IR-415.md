# R3-IR-415 — Sharp spin-half compression of physical Wilson vacuum curvature

Proved: at the last-neighbor decoupled SU(2) link, the compression of the target spin-one character to the real fundamental link sector has eigenvalues {1,-1/3,-1/3,-1/3}. Combined with both reduced-resolvent channels, this improves the curvature interval to -(44/27)(b_g/a_e)^2 <= c_1''(0) <= (20/9)(b_g/a_e)^2. These finite-volume inequalities are sign-sensitive but still admit negative curvature and do not give the full-coupling cap comparison.
