# R3-IR-426 — Small-cap electric capacity obstruction

Proved on SU(2): smooth strictly positive normalized center-even wavefunctions can have cap probability an arbitrarily small fraction of Haar while their electric Dirichlet energy is O(a) for caps of angular radius a. Consequently even vanishing kinetic energy at this scale, and in particular R3-IR-425's full-coupling local electric budget alone, does not imply uniform half-Haar shrinking-cap survival.
