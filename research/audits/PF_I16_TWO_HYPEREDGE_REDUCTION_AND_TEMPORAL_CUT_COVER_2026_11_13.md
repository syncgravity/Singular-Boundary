# PF-I16: two-hyperedge reduction and temporal cut-cover lemma

**Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Status:** exact reduction and termwise temporal-span lemma established; cancellation-preserving summation remains open  
**Scope:** finite-regulator product cumulants only. No P0–P5 gate is promoted.

## 1. The activity integral is exactly the full product cumulant

Let \(H_1,H_2\subsetneq[r]\) be overlapping hyperedges with
\(H_1\cup H_2=[r]\). For row \(i\), let
\[
A_i=X_iY_i,\qquad
X_i=\prod_{e\in H_1:\,i\in I_e}Y_{i,e}^{(1)},\quad
Y_i=\prod_{e\in H_2:\,i\in I_e}Y_{i,e}^{(2)},
\]
with the two link processes independent. In the two-link specialization,
\[
M_e(x_e)=\sum_{B\subseteq H_e}
\mathbb E\!\left[\prod_{i\in B}Y_{i,e}\right]x_{B,e},
\quad
F_\lambda=\mathsf D\!\left(M_1[1+\lambda(M_2-1)]\right).
\]
The row-diagonal projection \(\mathsf D\) keeps precisely those slot monomials that select either every incident slot of a row or none.

At \(\lambda=1\), the full-row moments are recovered, hence
\[
[z_{[r]}]\log F_1=\kappa(A_1,\ldots,A_r).
\]
At \(\lambda=0\), \(F_0=\mathsf D(M_1)\) depends only on row variables indexed by \(H_1\). Since \(H_1\subsetneq[r]\), it is independent of at least one \(z_j\), and therefore
\[
[z_{[r]}]\log F_0=0.
\]
The exact activity derivative identity consequently gives
\[
\boxed{\;
\int_0^1[z_{[r]}](\partial_\lambda F_\lambda)F_\lambda^{-1}\,d\lambda
=\kappa(A_1,\ldots,A_r).
\;}
\]
This is coefficientwise algebra in the finite nilpotent row algebra. In particular, the W2-tree estimate is precisely a normalized, time-integrated temporal tree bound for the *full product cumulant*. The interpolation is an exact representation, not by itself a new analytic gain.

## 2. Temporal cut-cover lemma for each connected partition term

Use the exact product-cumulant partition identity. For each link \(e\), choose a set partition \(\pi_e\) of its incident row set \(I_e\). A term contributes only when the join of the equivalence relations generated by all blocks of all \(\pi_e\), with rows outside \(I_e\) treated as singletons for that link, is the one-block partition of \([r]\). Its weight is
\[
\prod_e\prod_{B\in\pi_e}
\kappa_e(Y_{i,e}:i\in B).
\]
Singleton blocks are retained; they have no temporal-decay factor.

Let \(t_1,\ldots,t_r\) be arbitrary row times and write
\(t_{(1)}\le\cdots\le t_{(r)}\) for their increasing rearrangement. For every gap
\(g_j=t_{(j+1)}-t_{(j)}\), connectedness of the join implies that at least one block \(B\) of at least one \(\pi_e\) intersects both sides of this temporal cut. Otherwise no generated equivalence could connect a row on the left to a row on the right, contradicting that the join is one block. Such a crossing block has \(|B|\ge2\), and its temporal span
\[
\operatorname{span}(B)=\max_{i\in B}t_i-\min_{i\in B}t_i
\]
contains the gap \(g_j\).

Summing over all non-singleton blocks, every temporal gap is therefore counted at least once:
\[
\boxed{\;
\sum_e\sum_{\substack{B\in\pi_e\\|B|\ge2}}
\operatorname{span}(B)
\ \ge\ \sum_{j=1}^{r-1}g_j
\ =\ \max_i t_i-\min_i t_i.
\;}
\]
If the local link cumulant estimate is
\[
|\kappa_e(Y_{i,e}:i\in B)|
\le a_e(B)\,e^{-\Delta\,\operatorname{span}(B)}
\qquad(|B|\ge2),
\]
then each individual connected partition term has the common temporal factor
\[
\prod_e\prod_{\substack{B\in\pi_e\\|B|\ge2}}
e^{-\Delta\,\operatorname{span}(B)}
\le e^{-\Delta(\max_i t_i-\min_i t_i)}.
\]
Thus the one-span decay is a consequence of connectedness, not of a special choice of interpolation order.

## 3. What this does and does not solve

**Established exactly**
1. W2's activity-integrated coefficient equals the full product cumulant whenever \(H_1,H_2\) are proper, overlapping, and cover all rows.
2. Every connected term in the link-partition representation has at least one local cumulant block crossing every temporal cut.
3. The product of local exponential factors in each such term dominates one global maximum-span factor at the same rate \(\Delta\).

**Still open**
1. Summing the absolute values of connected partition terms with a \(C^r r!\)-type prefactor is not justified. The degree-eight cancellation witness shows that termwise absolute LS/partition majorants can be catastrophically large even when the final cumulant is modest.
2. A cancellation-preserving representation or bound is still needed to show that the *sum* of connected terms has a \(C^r\) normalized coefficient bound compatible with the explicit spanning-tree norm.
3. Stability under summing the link supports subject to \(\sum_e|I_e|\le8r\) remains open.

The cut-cover lemma supplies the temporal geometry for a future tree estimate, but does not establish W2-tree. It is not a P3 proof and does not promote any Observatory check.
