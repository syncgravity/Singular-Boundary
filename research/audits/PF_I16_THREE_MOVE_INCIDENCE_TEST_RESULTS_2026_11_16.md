## Conclusion

The proposed sufficient condition
\[
d_*\ge 3^{s-1},\qquad I(T)\le 3^{s-1}
\]
is already false at six ports: the second inequality fails, since \(14>9\). At eight ports it fails substantially, \(252>27\). This does **not** disprove the three-move uniform fractional transport: the exact weighted column maxima remain at most one at all tested orders (2/3, 4/5, and approximately 0.976422959218).

The useful lesson is that unweighted incidence is too coarse. High-incidence targets are reached disproportionately by sources with larger candidate sets, so the reciprocal degree weights matter. The next analytic target should bound
\[
W(T)=\sum_{x:T\in C(x)}\frac1{|C(x)|}
\]
directly, or establish a sharper joint incidence/degree inequality; the simple product of a global incidence maximum and a global minimum degree cannot do it.

## Reproducibility and proof boundary

The verifier's output is deterministic and uses exact rational arithmetic for the column loads. The n=8 decimal is a display of an exact rational accumulator. The run completed through eight ports. No n=10 computation, all-orders transport theorem, weighted one-link inequality, or Yang--Mills mass-gap proof is claimed.
