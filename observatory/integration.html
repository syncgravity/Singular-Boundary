    The conditional bridge that ties the gates together

    Elementary spectral implication · proved conditionally, not for Yang–Mills

    For a dense vacuum-centered family of reconstructed gauge-invariant states ψ, suppose their interacting positive-time self-correlations satisfy, with one physical decay rate M > 0 independent of lattice spacing and volume,

    0 ≤ ⟨ψ, e−tHψ⟩ ≤ Cψ e−Mt Cψ may depend on ψ; M must not depend on h, spatial box or the continuum sequence.

    Positivity and the spectral theorem imply μψ([0,a]) ≤ Cψ exp[−(M−a)t] for every a < M. Letting physical time t → ∞ excludes all excitation spectral weight below M. Density extends the exclusion to the physical Hilbert space.

    The missing mathematical advance is the bound itself, together with its compatible nontrivial continuum OS reconstruction. P-78's positive first moment and the newer source-visible positive-frequency weight do not imply this bound.
