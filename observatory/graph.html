A READER’S PATH

Two problems. Six transfer interfaces.

The cubic route is a constructive SU(2) control. Penrose geometry adds a distinct question about quasicrystalline operators. A proof on cubic does not automatically transfer to another regulator.

01

Understand what is proved

Finite cubic refinement, Gauss-law operators and transfer positivity are scoped results. The finite strong-coupling gap bound does not apply along the asymptotically free continuum coupling trajectory.

02

Follow T0–T5

T0: physical 3D geometry; T1: Gauss action; T2: renormalized probes; T3: interacting time correlations; T4: nontrivial OS limit; T5: spectral transfer. Every interface remains open or conditional.

03

Separate quantum gravity

Graph spectral dimension, zeta regularization, and the physical gravitational fluctuation operator are distinct. The gravitational ultraviolet claim needs an independent operator and continuum argument.

01

Prove the exact interface

Every arrow names a dependency or a candidate bridge. The required lemma must use the same model, physical units, domain, and quantifiers at both ends.

02

Preserve the record

Source files carry SHA-256 digests and Library custody paths. Git stores curated metadata, verification scripts and proofs; binary originals await Git LFS.

03

Review before closing
