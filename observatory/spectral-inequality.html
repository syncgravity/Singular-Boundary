The central theorem

For each source-derived physical regulator at refinement h and physical volume R, let Ωh,R be its true interacting Gauss-invariant ground state and dνh,R = |Ωh,R|²dU. With physical electric coefficients αe,h,R and the derivative metric fixed by the same Hamiltonian, prove:

Varνh,R(f) ≤ (1 / m*) Σe αe,h,R ∫ |∇ef|² dνh,R

for every gauge-invariant f, with m* > 0 independent of h and R in physical units.

The exact finite ground-state transform identifies the Rayleigh quotient with the Dirichlet form. It does not make the lower bound uniform. Measure, generator, coefficients, metric, boundary conditions and physical time must be the same in every imported estimate.
