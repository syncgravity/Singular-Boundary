How to read the evidence

The governing rule is transfer discipline: an estimate is useful only when its assumptions, measure, generator, geometry, normalization and limits match the target theorem.

Exact finite result means proved at the stated finite regulator. Conditional means the stated hypotheses remain obligations. Control-model result means it is not source-native. Obstruction is retained as a constraint on future constructions. Open means the required transfer or theorem has not been established.

No theorem is promoted by repetition, numerical agreement, a successful finite computation, or inclusion in the source index. Negative results and failed approaches remain part of the proof record.
