### D. R3-IR and cubic/other regulator lanes

R3-IR, cubic SU(2), prism, and related audits are separate model lanes. They can provide conditional comparison estimates, finite reference results, obstructions, and verifier techniques. They must not be relabelled as the Penrose-source physical regulator without a proved equivalence map.

The PR review board and individual theorem dossiers define each lane's scope. In particular, an R3-IR finite-strip Hessian estimate is not automatically a PF-I16 source-native vacuum estimate.

### E. Canonical Yang–Mills proof objective

The canonical missing result is one source-compatible, physically normalized, interacting-vacuum, refinement- and physical-volume-uniform gauge-invariant Poincaré inequality. It must use the same authenticated source-native Hamiltonian, physical electric coefficients, metric, time normalization, Gauss constraint, and true vacuum throughout. Only after this can the program claim the required infrared exclusion and nontrivial four-dimensional continuum reconstruction.

All canonical proof gates remain OPEN. Finite exact results, conditional inequalities, numerical evidence, and auxiliary models must not be promoted to a physical continuum mass-gap theorem.

## Evidence and status vocabulary

Use these labels consistently in new and revised records:

- **source-definition** — directly specified by the identified source construction or manuscript.
- **source-replay** — regenerated from authenticated source inputs with recorded hashes and parameters.
- **exact-finite** — proved exactly for the stated finite object.
- **independent-mathematics** — a separate derivation/control not yet identified with the source construction.
- **numerical-finite** — computed finite result; state deterministic/stochastic method and uncertainty.
- **conditional** — valid only under listed hypotheses or externally chosen parameters.
- **historical-reconstruction** — recovered author intent or prior workflow, not a verbatim source or proof.
- **obstruction / negative result** — a proved limitation or counterexample under explicit hypotheses.
- **open** — required construction or proof not supplied.

A result may carry more than one label. Never let a label such as “verified” stand alone without saying what was verified, on which object, and by what method.

## Required structure for new research records

Every new theorem/audit dossier should include:

1. Stable claim ID and exact mathematical statement.
2. Source identity: paper/version, file path, commit/blob SHA or archive SHA-256.
3. Model identity and explicit exclusions (source graph vs completed control vs cubic regulator, etc.).
4. Hypotheses, conventions, units, normalization, and boundary conditions.
5. Proof or reproducible computation, including command and verifier/test paths.
6. Exact/numerical/conditional status and uncertainty/sample-independence details.
7. Counterexamples, failed approaches, and superseded claims.
8. Dependencies and downstream claims that may or may not use the result.
9. Remaining proof obligations and next smallest decisive test.
10. Observatory concept/node link where applicable.

## Maintenance rules

- Do not create a new branch for each review. Use the existing canonical integration PR or an appropriate existing workstream branch after inspecting its scope.
- Do not merge unrelated mathematical models merely to reduce branch count. Integrate their *reviewed source records* into main with explicit model labels and provenance.
- Do not delete historical research or negative results. Supersede with cross-links and a correction note.
- Keep generated catalogs and graph data synchronized through their declared generator/source workflow; run the relevant integrity checks.
- Treat the live theorem registry as a file inventory, not as a claim that every indexed theorem has been independently re-proved.
- Keep the concept index, source dossiers, verifier, tests, and evidence mutually linked.
- Update the root README's entry points and this map whenever a new primary workstream or canonical proof target is added.
