## Practical definition of progress

**Tool advancement:** a faster MPO/Schatten algorithm, an improved lattice simulation, an additional source-recovery step or a stronger fixed-volume inequality. Worth recording; not by itself a continuum theorem advance.

**Gate advancement:** a theorem with the *correct 3D model* and one more previously missing quantifier or physical normalization (time/volume/refinement/coupling/gauge sector) closed, including proof and independent reproducibility.

**Final advancement:** physical h→0 and R→∞ nontrivial interacting Schwinger functions, common positive exponential decay in physical time for a dense observable algebra, OS reconstruction and an actual nonzero continuum spectral gap. A uniform bare gap that becomes 0 in physical units is not success.

**Editorial governance:** keep existing v2.24 public-facing source at main untouched pending independent review; an Integration view may be maintained on a *draft branch* and is **not** automatically deployed. Freeze canonical claims and legacy indices until the acceptance protocol permits changes; never reassign legacy claim IDs or replace the March dissertation in place. Publish finite results with carefully scoped titles before a full constructive claim.
