# Artifact preservation requirement

Never declare computational research complete until original inputs and generated numerical artifacts have been saved to durable storage and independently retrieved. Record stable location, SHA-256, size, and provenance. Temporary /mnt/data paths are not permanent storage. Never replace an inaccessible historical matrix with a reconstruction without labeling it as different. Review this requirement at the start of every new research session. The lost-access Whitney matrix incident motivated this rule.
